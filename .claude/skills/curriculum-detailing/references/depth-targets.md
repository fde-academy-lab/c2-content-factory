# Depth targets

A table of contents says which topic. It never says how deep. The depth target
is the missing number, set per sub-topic per session, and it rises as the
course goes on.

This is content depth. It is a different thing from the process depth dial in
`build-contract` (Direct, Shaped, Programme), which sizes how much process a
piece of work gets. Do not use one word for both.

## The four levels

| Level | The learner can | What the material must contain | Interview question it answers |
|---|---|---|---|
| **L1 Name it** | Recognise it, define it in plain words, say where it is used and why | Grounding, the pain, the clearest example, the crux line | What is X and where would you use it |
| **L2 Use it** | Run the steps on a clean case and get the right answer | The steps numbered, one worked example with values, the applied slide on the spine case | Given this situation, what do you do |
| **L3 Explain why** | Derive it from first principles, say why it has this shape, say why it fails | The strip to first principles, the framework derived not presented, the gotcha, the anti-pattern | Why does this work, why is it built this way, why does it fail |
| **L4 Handle it breaking** | Diagnose it live, choose between alternatives with a threshold, design with it | The failure slide, the deliberate failure in the code file with its trace, the decision rule, the comparison | It broke like this, what do you check first. What would make you choose the other option |

Names are chosen to decode themselves. Do not rename them per course.

## How each level weights the ten-slot unit

Every major concept still runs the full unit. The level decides where the
minutes go.

| Slot | L1 | L2 | L3 | L4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 The pain | full | full | one line | one line |
| 2 Ground it | full | short | recall | recall |
| 3 Strip to first principles | skip | one line | full | full, and the room does it |
| 4 The clearest example | full | full | short | recall |
| 5 The framework, derived | presented | presented | derived | derived, and the room does it |
| 6 The steps | shown | full | full | full |
| 7 One worked example | shown | full | full | learner completes it |
| 8 Apply to the spine case | short | full | full | full |
| 9 The gotcha | one line | short | full | full, with a second failure |
| 10 Close: crux, steps, transfer | crux only | crux and steps | all three | all three, and the two follow-ups |

"Recall" means one line and a backward hook to the session where it was
taught. "Skip" means the slot is absent from this session and present when the
idea returns.

## Rising altitude across the course

Static depth is futile. A room that has built four of something no longer
needs the library analogy, and giving it to them slows the session and mildly
insults them. That is the expertise reversal effect, and it is established.

The arc below uses the same three bands as the study notes, so the two
artifacts move together.

| | Early, first quarter | Middle | Late, final quarter |
|---|---|---|---|
| First encounter with an idea | L1 or L2 | L2 | L2, and L3 within the same session for the confident half |
| Revisit | Rises by one | Rises by one | Reaches L4 for every core idea |
| Example order | Clearest example first, spine case second | Spine case first, clearest example as a one-line recall | Spine case only, and a second world for transfer |
| Question form | Answerable from ordinary experience | Needs a framework from two sessions back | Combines two strands, or asks what would make the opposite choice right |
| Guidance | Full worked example, every step with values | Complete-the-missing-step | Build from a brief, the trainer reviews |
| Failure content | One gotcha, named | The gotcha and how to read the trace | Two failures, and the learner diagnoses one live |
| Case studies | Company, number, consequence | Company, number, what they changed | Company, number, and one source that disagrees |

Record which band a session is in on its row. The checker reads it.

## The rules that follow

1. **The first encounter is never L3 or L4.** Depth without a foundation is the
   ten-thousand-feet failure in disguise: it sounds deep and lands as gyaan
2. **A revisited idea rises at least one level.** If it cannot rise, it is a
   recall, not a revisit, and it costs one line
3. **Every core idea reaches L4 before the course ends.** Core means the ideas
   the transfer questions of the final quarter depend on. Name them at the map
   stage
4. **Depth is set by where the room is, not by where the trainer is.** A trainer
   comfortable at L4 will teach L4 to an L1 room unless the row says otherwise.
   The row says otherwise

## The return question

Depth is built by being questioned repeatedly. Every session's starter quiz
carries one return question: a transfer question from an earlier session,
asked one depth level higher than when it was first asked.

| First asked at | Returns as |
|---|---|
| L1: what is retrieval and where would you use it | L2: your bot answers wrongly one time in five, do you retrieve better or train more |
| L2: retrieve better or train more | L3: why does a fine-tune not fix a stale fact |
| L3: why does a fine-tune not fix a stale fact | L4: the wiki grew to thirty thousand pages and answers got worse, what do you check first |

The return question does three jobs at once: spaced retrieval, a backward hook
to the earlier session, and a live reading of whether the room actually rose.

## Guidance fading

The sequence from full worked example, to completing a partly worked one, to
solving from a brief is established in cognitive load research and it is the
mechanism behind the guidance row above. Applied here:

- **Early**: the worked example on the slide carries every step and every value.
  The mid-session exercise asks the learner to run the same steps on a case that
  differs by one number
- **Middle**: the worked example on the slide stops one step short. The room
  completes it. The exercise gives the case and two of five steps
- **Late**: the slide gives the brief and the answer. The learner reconstructs
  the steps. The exercise gives only the brief, and the trainer reviews

A course that keeps early-band guidance in its final quarter has trained the
room to wait for the worked example. That room fails the interview question
that starts with a blank page.
