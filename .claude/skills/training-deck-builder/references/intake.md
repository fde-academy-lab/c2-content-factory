# Intake: what to establish before building

## Search memory first, always

Before asking anything, look for the cohort in memory and in past
conversations. Most of this list is usually already known.

- Who is in the room: roles, seniority, years, the split between technical
  and business
- What they already know and what they rate themselves low on
- What landed and what failed in earlier sessions of the same programme
- The running case already in use, and the named devices already taught
- The design system already approved for this client
- The client's own vocabulary and the terms they use differently
- Whether the trainer is a domain expert here or learning it while delivering

That last one changes the build. When the trainer is at partial domain
mastery: one idea per slide, the slide text is what they say, and anywhere
deep domain knowledge is needed the slide asks the room instead of asserting.

## Ask at most three questions, as choices

Only for what memory cannot answer. Phrase as options, not open questions.

1. **The slot.** How long is the session, and does this deck fill all of it
   or one half?
2. **The room.** Mostly practitioners, mostly leadership, or genuinely mixed?
3. **The anchor.** Is there an existing running case to continue, or does
   this need a fresh one?

Secondary, only when they change the build materially:

- Is there a curriculum or content file to follow, and is its spine fixed?
- Hands-on: none, demo only, or learners at keyboards?
- Anything from the previous session that must be picked up or repaired?

## Defaults when the user does not answer

| Unknown | Assume |
|---|---|
| Session length | Four hours, one break, two decks of roughly 105 minutes |
| Slides per deck | 25 to 34 |
| Room | Mixed seniority, so every concept starts from scratch and every table carries a depth row |
| Running case | Continue the existing one if memory has one, otherwise invent one everyday case and thread it through |
| Activity | One block, 10 to 15 minutes, selection-based |
| Opening | Three or four questions, each planting the topic it precedes |
| Vendor specificity | Method stays vendor-neutral, the client stack appears once as the applied example near the end |

## The arithmetic, written down in the reply

Total minutes, minus break and wrap, equals live teaching minutes. Divide by
the slide count at 3 to 3.5 minutes per teaching slide, under a minute for a
glance picture, and activity time at its stated duration. If the total
overruns, say so before building and name what would be cut.
