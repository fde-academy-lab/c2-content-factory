Build weeks (shaded): no new content. Mini-project on a named-customer brief, mock interview with half the time as a project viva, impromptu group discussion, offline presentation and a live demo. All grades close in-week.

Build 3 is the one exception to 'no new content': a guided NLP track of three short clinics (text preparation, TF-IDF and a no-LLM baseline, first prompt patterns) runs inside the build, the project uses it, and it carries no test.

Week 16 (blue) opens with Major Exam 3 in the first half of Monday and is then the Forward Deployed Engineering block, roughly 27 hours: a day and a half of client discovery and design, one day of solution architecture with the assessed solution proposal, two days of deployment (Docker, CI/CD, rollback, upgrade), one day of AI security, responsible AI and testing. The capstone is announced at the week's end.

Forward deployed thinking runs as one thread. Week 0 seeds problem framing and solution thinking. Each build week drills one move: Build 1 framing in a new domain, Build 2 trade-offs under unequal error costs, Build 3 the no-LLM option beside the LLM, Build 4 quantified option evaluation, Build 5 the POC-against-MVP pitch. Week 16 runs design to deployment.

Capstone (Weeks 17 to 20, Module 10, 60 hours): different groups receive different briefs by theme area, and one plan with three gates fits every brief: the proposal and a walking skeleton in Week 17, the measured core in Week 18, the deployed and documented system in Week 19. The live demo and panel defence run in the first half of Week 20, followed by closure. The plan is in the 'W17-20 Capstone' tab.

Placement does not wait for Week 20. It runs integrated from Week 0, adds dedicated work from month two (CV and LinkedIn, role-track declaration, positioning, behavioural coaching, a readiness gate in Week 14), and goes live with interviews from Week 15 at the latest. The 'Placement beat' column carries it week by week.

Week 0 (green) is the in-person baseline week before teaching starts. It carries no marks and sits outside the 510 hours and 33 credits, which stay mapped to Weeks 1 to 20.

Machine learning essentials are supported by a pre-work bridge course, so classroom time in Wk5 weights toward doubt-clearing and application. Computer Vision is deliberately excluded.

Major exams (yellow in the week number and focus cells): ME1 in Week 5, ME2 in Week 10, and ME3 in the first half of Week 16 Monday. ME3 left Week 15 on 21 September, so Build 5 carries only its mini project, mock interview and GD.

IITGN faculty sessions (violet): about 60 hours as 30 sessions of 120 minutes, in seven teaching weeks that end with retrieval in Week 11. Within its week the block runs on every teaching day, after the day's applied core, and picks up where the trainer's row stops. Every session is tentative until IITGN confirms the faculty member and the dates; the sessions themselves are in the violet column of the weekly tabs. The session counts are inputs in blue, and the hours and the totals are formulas.
