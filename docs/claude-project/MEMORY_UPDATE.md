Update this project's memory with the teaching approach as the requester completed it on 13 October
2026. It applies to every week, day, Saturday and project built from 12 October 2026 on. Keep
people's names out of memory: roles only.

Remember:

- A teaching day has two halves, and the topics come first. The morning opens on the day's topic, the
  week map, and the topics slide and the concept map, which name every subtopic of the curriculum row,
  verbatim and in the row's order, each with the chapter that teaches it; then the case ticket in one
  slide, as the destination. The concept block follows, 60 to 120 minutes: one chapter per subtopic,
  in the row's order unless the week's spine records a reason, its opener titled with the subtopic as
  the row words it and then a short question, taught from zero on a simple, complete, relatable
  educational example with invented numbers (a canteen that pays per token, a hostel canteen's twelve
  lunch orders, a neighbourhood gym's monthly joiners, a chai stall's cups), climbing from the misconception and a
  prediction through the hand count and the picture to the formula or rule named last, with three to
  five rules and a map node, and paired with one short notebook of the same name, which loads the
  example's table from the day's data and prints it in full. A concept chapter may open the
  afternoon, before the case chapter that applies it.
- Then the case carries the day. An engineering case at a company the learners use, such as Uber,
  Ola, Swiggy, Zomato, Blinkit, Zepto, Flipkart, Amazon India, PhonePe, Google or an AI startup, in a
  named city, state or country, with the metric, its value before and after with dates, the data the
  analyst holds, the ask and the known caveats, labelled illustrative (the company is real, the
  situation and the data are synthetic), claiming nothing the company did, every public fact from a
  dated source, and no stakeholder drama. It is climbed in three or four case chapters that each name
  the subtopics they apply, each paired with a notebook.
- Kalpa stays the programme's client: the TA lab and one unguided exercise run the same method on
  Kalpa's version of the problem, which keeps the weeks connected and the Saturday bank in reach.
- One notebook per chapter, in both halves, named as the chapter is: a concept notebook by its
  subtopic, a case notebook by its step with the subtopics it applies in its first cell.
- Each case climbs a ladder from a weak learner to a hard interview: L1 a hand count on about twelve
  rows, L2 the full data in the smallest code, L3 the trap and the denominator, L4 the caveats an
  engineer must name, L5 the decision with its cost and the objection, L6 the interview at Uber,
  Swiggy, Google and AI-startup depth with weak and hire-grade answers side by side and an
  AI-company variant.
- Every deck, notebook, exercise, case study, project, Saturday paper and note teaches in a blend of
  styles: Veritasium's misconception and prediction first; 3Blue1Brown's and StatQuest's visual
  derivation of the maths from tiny numbers before the formula; Krish Naik's line-by-line code-along to
  a working result; ByteByteGo's connected maps; takeUforward's ladder of problems from easy to
  interview grade; Brilliant's problem-first interactivity; the Feynman technique; plus retrieval of
  earlier days, interleaving, fading worked examples, wrong answers to debug, and peer instruction.
- Each week ships one interactive HTML explorer that grows day by day. Pictures are drawn by our own
  code: mermaid for mechanisms and maps, matplotlib for data pictures, short GIFs with a still beside each.
- Every teaching day ships a coverage matrix: each row subtopic, verbatim, against its chapter, slides,
  notebook, exercise items, Kahoot items and minutes. A script fails a pack where a subtopic has no
  chapter or no notebook carrying its name. The curriculum row's Subtopics column is the day's chapter
  list and the contract with the institute and the learner.
- A redirection is recorded as a diff against the standing rules, never as a new text: every decision
  says what it keeps and what it drops, and the approval screen prints the dropped lines.
- Review from Week 4 on is three cold reads (a fresh graduate, a hiring interviewer, and a trainer who
  did not write the day reading with the row open), one fix pass and the verify command.
- Week 4 (26 to 31 October 2026) is the first week built this way: Uber Hyderabad for metric design
  and its stakeholder defence, Swiggy Instamart Pune for basket analysis, Zomato Gold Chennai for cohorts
  and funnels, a Flipkart fulfilment centre in Lucknow for forecasting across the Diwali shift, and a
  Saturday of a 120-minute paper, a transfer drill and paired mock interviews; its concept blocks were
  added on 13 October 2026 after the requester read the decks.

Forget or correct:

- "The case leads" as the first thing a day shows, and "a pack whose deck opens on a topic title was
  not built from the row": the topics lead the morning, the case carries the day, and the test is
  now that every chapter and notebook carries the row's subtopic it teaches or applies.
- The 12 October instruction to forget "about six chapters a day": only the count and the five-pass
  loop were dropped; one deck chapter paired with one notebook, the problem before the code, a second
  route and a third of items as design items stay for every week.
- A teaching day that opens on a stakeholder's quote such as "Meera wants one number", for any week
  from Week 4 on; Weeks 1 to 3 keep that shape until they are rebuilt.
- Fixed quotas of slides, items and words; diagrams limited to mermaid fences.
