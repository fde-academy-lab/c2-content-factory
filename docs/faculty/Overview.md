Design of the faculty sessions
1. | Each session runs for 120 minutes and follows that day's applied session, which an industry trainer teaches on the same topic through a running business case.
2. | The faculty session then carries the theory beneath the method the learners have just used: the derivation, the mathematics and the conditions under which the method holds. Theory therefore arrives once the learners have a reason to want it.
3. | Sessions run on consecutive teaching days within a week, in seven weeks between Week 2 and Week 11. From Week 13 the programme moves to agentic systems, deployment and the capstone, which are taught by industry practitioners.
