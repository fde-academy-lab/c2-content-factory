# Structure

IITGN COHORT 2  ·  PROGRAMME STRUCTURE AND ASSESSMENT  ·  COMPONENTS LOCK IN HERE AS THEY ARE DECIDED
Component | Status | Detail
Week types | Locked | Three kinds of week. Regular teaching weeks (1, 2, 4, 5, 7, 8 in this workbook) run the daily teaching shape plus the Saturday recap block. Build weeks (3, 6, 9, then 12 and 15) run the mini project and carry no tests. Capstone weeks begin later in the programme per the phase map.
Regular-week Saturday | Locked | A pen-and-paper weekly recap test, AI-free, about two hours, then a break, then the solution discussion. The paper is derived from the week's interview question set and framed for short answers so papers can be swapped for peer cross-evaluation; the Academic TA leads the solution discussion as an interview-answer discussion with random call-outs. Ungraded: a performance indicator, never a marks component.
Interview question sets | Locked (format) | Each regular week's Saturday row carries the question set, questions only; answers are written at the detailing phase. Tags: [S] staple asked everywhere · [F] frequent in GCC and product screens · [SV] service-major screen opener · [D] differentiator. The tagging is this programme's own calibration for 0-3 year Indian-market candidates.
Major exams | Locked | ME1 in Week 5 (50 marks), ME2 in Week 10 (100 marks), ME3 in Week 15 (150 marks). Each runs during its week as a continuous activity; the exact day and slot are not fixed yet, so no artifact states one.
Assessment pool | Partly locked | Total 1000 marks. Graded components: GD score, mini project score (including the presentation and other elements if required), mock interview score, major exams (300 across the three), capstone. The exact split of the remaining 700 is pending, so no artifact states a weight or a percentage until it locks.
Ungraded indicators | Locked | Daily Kahoot quizzes and the Saturday recap test carry no marks; both are read as performance indicators.
Build-week anatomy | Locked (Build 1) | Monday: the Programme Head introduces the mini projects online. Groups of four for the mini project, the GDs and the presentations. Five client-zero sub-problems per build week, three groups per sub-problem, so the panel hears alternate viewpoints on the same business problem. Build Monday to Thursday with the trainer's parallel build and daily checkpoints. The industry expert attends Friday and Saturday only, running GDs at about 30 minutes per group plus some project presentations; a senior industry leader flies in on Saturday, same-day return, for presentations. All grades close in-week. Build 1 mocks: a share with the Principal Advisor, the rest split between the Programme Head and the Academic TA. Build 2 and Build 3 staffing is not yet detailed.
Client zero narrative | Frame locked | One large global MNC with several business units across different domains. The teaching spine (Weeks 1 to 9 so far) lives inside one vertical, and every build week draws its five sub-problems from across the verticals, each morphed from an interview-classic case study into the client-zero persona. The intent is domain maturity: by the capstone, a learner has worked the same company from five directions. The company name and entity model still need the formal lock before build-week briefs are written.
Confirmed calendar | Locked | Gandhi Jayanti Friday 2 October (Week 1). Dussehra Tuesday 20 October (Week 4). Diwali Sunday 8 November with Monday 9 November also off (Week 7 teaches Tuesday to Friday). Guru Nanak Jayanti Tuesday 24 November (Week 9). All dates per the gazetted 2026 list; the institute's own calendar remains the final word on any additional local holiday.
