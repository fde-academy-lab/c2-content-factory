Week 0: the week before teaching starts
Week 0 starts (Monday) | Mon 28 Sep 2026 | Derived from the Week 1 start date on the Journey tab. Half-day blocks are shown; the institute publishes the exact slots.
Day | Date | First half | Second half
Monday | Mon 28 Sep 2026 | Arrival, document verification and welcome, run by the institute. | Programme orientation, about three hours: your 20 weeks, how a week runs, your client, the tools, then accounts and workspace setup. You also rate yourself on each baseline topic.
Tuesday | Tue 29 Sep 2026 | Institute address and orientation. | Baseline check on paper, about two hours, no AI: Python, SQL, statistics and problem articulation. A ten-minute briefing for Wednesday's introductions follows.
Wednesday | Wed 30 Sep 2026 | Python brush-up, part one, about three hours: the Python that Week 1 uses (values, loops, records as dictionaries, functions, files, reading a traceback, count, sum, mean and median). | Introduce yourself to the cohort, about three hours for the room: four minutes each, two on you and two on one thing you built and what broke in it.
Thursday | Thu 01 Oct 2026 | Python brush-up, part two, into SQL, about two hours, then a one-hour class on articulating a problem before solving it. | SQL brush-up, about three hours: the SQL that Week 2 starts from (SELECT, WHERE, GROUP BY, HAVING, ORDER BY, and running a query from VS Code against Postgres).
Friday | Fri 02 Oct 2026 | Gandhi Jayanti. No session. | An optional self-paced practice set is available.
Saturday | Sat 03 Oct 2026 | Session with a working Forward Deployed Engineer, about four hours and up to six. | The Saturday session runs as one block.
The baseline check | It has three steps: on Monday you rate yourself on each topic, on Tuesday you sit the paper, and on Wednesday or Thursday a ten-minute one-to-one with a TA, during lab time, agrees your starting point. It carries no marks.
Why the brush-up | Week 1 runs on Python from its first hour and Week 2 runs on SQL, so this week brings everyone to the same starting line.
Saturday | A working Forward Deployed Engineer spends the session on the job itself and on your questions. Every build week in the programme is run the way a forward deployed engineer would run it.
By Saturday evening | Your accounts and workspace are set up, so Week 1 opens on the work itself. / Your starting point in Python, SQL and statistics is agreed and signed off with a TA. / You have written and run the Python that Week 1 uses and the SQL that Week 2 starts from. / You have met every member of the cohort and heard what each has built.
