# The one-to-one guide: the baseline card in ten minutes

TRAINER ONLY. For the TA running a one-to-one on Wednesday or Thursday, beside the practice track or
during lab time.

## Bring

The learner's folder: Monday's self-rating form, Tuesday's four marked papers and a blank baseline
card, plus the line for the card from the last column of the score workbook's Scores sheet.

## The ten minutes

| Duration | Move | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 2 min | Their read first | The learner says which paper felt hardest and why, before any count is shown |
| 3 min | The two columns | The card's table is filled from the form and the workbook, and the two of you find the topic where the columns disagree most, with the items behind it on the table |
| 3 min | What they can do alone | One line per topic in the learner's words, naming tasks they can do today |
| 2 min | Two actions, each with a date | One action on the widest gap and one on the Python Week 1 Monday opens with, each with how the learner will know it is done and by when |

Both of you sign the card by Saturday. It goes home with the learner, and the TA keeps a copy off the
repository.

## Three rules

1. Only the learner's own counts appear: never another learner's count, the track line, a level label
   or a comparison with the room. When a learner asks where they stand against the others, the
   honest answer is that no card shows anyone else's count.
2. The Monday letter is the learner's own claim and the Tuesday count is what one paper found. The
   card puts them side by side, and the conversation is about the gap between them.
3. The Python track is where Wednesday's brush-up started for this learner, and it moves when the
   ticks do. Say so if the learner reads it as a verdict.

## Actions that work, and actions that do not

| Works | Does not work |
|---|---|
| Finish the hands-on notebook with every check passing, by Friday | Practise more Python |
| Watch videos 4, 5 and 7 of the row's Python playlist and redo items 3 and 5 of Tuesday's paper from a blank notebook, by Sunday | Revise the basics |
| Write the librarian's three questions as three functions, run them on the next week's log, and check the answers against the notebook's, by Monday | Get better at functions |

An action works when someone else could check it: a named task, a finished state and a date.

## What to record

Note which learners have had their one-to-one and the two actions each agreed, on the TAs' own sheet
and never in the repository. Anyone left over carries to Thursday's lab time, and every card is
signed by Saturday.
