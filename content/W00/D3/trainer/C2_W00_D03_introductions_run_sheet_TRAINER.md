# The introductions: run sheet

TRAINER ONLY. For whoever keeps the clock and whoever keeps the capture sheet in the second half.

## Before the first speaker

1. Draw the speaking order at random from the day's attendance, write it into the capture sheet below
   and put it on the screen, so every learner knows when their turn comes.
2. Set a timer the timekeeper can see and have a card ready that reads "one minute".
3. Put the deck's half two on the projector, `slides/C2_W00_D03_half2_STUDENT.pptx`.

## The opening, 10 minutes

Show S1, the shape of the four minutes. Read the first version on D2 aloud, word for word, and ask the
room what an interviewer would ask about next. Take two answers, then show D3, the second version of
the same story, row by row. The point to land is the two places a project answer falls apart: the
speaker's own part, and what broke.

## The rounds

| When | What happens |
|---|---|
| Three minutes into a turn | The card goes up |
| Four minutes into a turn | The next speaker starts, finished or not |
| After the first half of the order | A ten-minute break |
| After the last speaker | Two minutes on what the room heard most often and least often |

If time runs short, cut the break first. Never cut a speaker's four minutes, and never skip a speaker.
