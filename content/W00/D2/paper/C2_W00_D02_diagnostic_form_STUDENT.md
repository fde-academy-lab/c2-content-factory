# The baseline diagnostic

Week 0, Tuesday. About 90 minutes, in one sitting, in your browser.

Open the form, checked 28 September 2026: https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSf50XTU4RN8eS7gOksWQcOYlqWaxGkSszqpG3JMV_rQD5YL2Q/viewform

If your laptop fails, ask for the paper copy, `C2_W00_D02_diagnostic_STUDENT.docx`: the same forty
questions with an answer sheet, which a TA types in for you afterwards.

---

## The rules

- Your own head only: no second tab, no notes and no AI assistant. Rough working on paper is fine.
- Every question has four options and one best answer, except Section E, where each situation asks
  for the best action and the worst action.
- Read code as written, and assume Python 3 and PostgreSQL unless a question says it is pseudocode.
- Answer every question. A blank answer tells the programme less than a wrong one.
- The paper is not graded and goes on no transcript.

## The first page

Your email address, your student ID and your full name, then a rating from 1 to 4 on five areas, as
you are today, before you see any question:

| Rating | What it means |
|---|---|
| 1 | I have not used this |
| 2 | I can follow it when someone shows me |
| 3 | I can do it alone on a small problem |
| 4 | I can find and fix mistakes in someone else's version |

## The five sections

| Section | Questions | About |
|---|---|---|
| A. Python for data and GenAI | Q1 to Q12 | 30 minutes |
| B. SQL for data and GenAI | Q13 to Q20 | 20 minutes |
| C. Numbers and reasoning | Q21 to Q28 | 14 minutes |
| D. Case and scenarios | Q29 to Q34 | 10 minutes |
| E. Judgment calls | Q35 to Q40 | 5 minutes |

The section times are a guide. Some questions are set inside Kalpa Retail, a fictional company you
will meet again from Week 1, and nothing about it needs to be known in advance.

## When you submit

Your score and the correct answers sit behind the form's "View score" link, and a full report with
an explanation for every question goes to the email address you typed. It cannot be resent, so type
the address carefully. Keep the email: your ten-minute one-to-one on Wednesday or Thursday starts
from it, and every question is discussed at length afterwards on GitHub Discussions.
