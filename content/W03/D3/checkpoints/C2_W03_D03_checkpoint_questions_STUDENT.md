# The daily checkpoint: three questions for your sub-problem

Your group answers its sub-problem's three questions in two minutes, from your own work, with the
numbers in front of you. One member speaks per question, and a different member each time. Every
answer is a number and the file it came from. A group that is still stuck says so here, today,
since tomorrow the build has to be nearly done.

Each question asks the same three things of a different sub-problem: what your first profile
showed, what you reconciled against what, and what your denominator is.

---

## Sub-problem 1. Where lab revenue comes from, and which branch is short

1. **Profile.** In the claims file, how many rows and how many distinct claim ids did you
   find, and which column would not convert to a number on your first try, and on how many rows?
2. **Reconcile.** Which count from the booking side did you check the claims against, and what
   was left over on each side once the two were matched?
3. **Denominator.** Name every average on your revenue tree, and for each one say what it is an
   average per, with that count for Q2 and for Q3.

## Sub-problem 2. Bookings fell in two metros in Q3

1. **Profile.** How many booking files did you open for the two metros, and for each one, how many
   rows and how many distinct booking ids does it hold?
2. **Reconcile.** What did you check the bookings against, metro by metro and quarter by quarter,
   and where did the two counts disagree?
3. **Denominator.** State the fall in the two metros as a Q2 count and a Q3 count, and name the
   file or files each count comes from.

## Sub-problem 3. Claims and remittances disagree

1. **Profile.** In the remittances file, what forms does the claim reference take, and how many rows
   carry each form?
2. **Reconcile.** What share of postings matched a claim on your first join, what did you change
   to raise it, and what is left unmatched on each side now?
3. **Denominator.** When you say "collected", which rows count as money received and which did you
   leave out, with the count and the dollars of each?

## Sub-problem 4. The no-show rate looks worse at one patient service centre

1. **Profile.** In the appointments file, how many rows does each site have, and what values does
   every column take?
2. **Reconcile.** What did you check the appointment counts against, and did the totals agree site
   by site?
3. **Denominator.** Give the centre's no-show rate and the rest's as numerator over denominator,
   and say which visits you counted in each denominator.

## Sub-problem 5. The free at-home collection campaign "lifted bookings 9 percent"

1. **Profile.** In the campaign file, how many patients were offered the collection, in which
   metros, over which dates, and how many took it up?
2. **Reconcile.** Did every patient in the campaign file turn up in the patients file and in the
   bookings, and what did you check to know?
3. **Denominator.** The 9 percent is bookings per what, over which window, compared with which
   patients? Say how the comparison group differs from the offered group.

---

**If your group cannot answer one of its three,** say which one and what is in the way. That is a
blocker, and naming it now gets the Academic TA to your table in the open build time.
