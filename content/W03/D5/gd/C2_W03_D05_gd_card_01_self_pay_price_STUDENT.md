# GD card 01: Should Kalpa Health cut the self-pay price of its Whole-body wellness panel from $299 to $249?

Build 1, Friday's group discussion. Read the card alone in three minutes; the rules and the scoring
are at its end. Kalpa Health and its people are fictional, and the fact about a real company was
checked on 1 October 2026.

> "Marketing wants the Whole-body wellness panel at $249 in January for patients who pay for it
> themselves, down from $299. Do we do it? Give me the one number that decides it."
> Dr Priya Menon, COO, Kalpa Health

**Who needs the answer.** Dr Menon, who signs January's prices next week. A cut that brings no new
buyers gives away $50 on every panel Kalpa would have sold anyway.

**The questions on the way.** Who pays this price? What does a panel earn at each price, and how
many more must the cut sell? What is each head counting? What do you owe Dr Menon when time is
called?

## Who pays this price, and why is it on the table now?

**Who needs the answer.** The marketing head, who briefs a January campaign on Monday.

**The questions on the way.** What is a self-pay patient? Does a real lab sell tests this way?

A self-pay patient pays Kalpa directly instead of through a health plan, because they have no plan
or their plan will not pay for this panel, so Kalpa's list price is their price. January is when
people want their numbers checked. Quest Diagnostics, a US lab company with about 2,400 patient
service centres, sells tests straight to consumers at QuestHealth.com, which its annual report
calls consumer-initiated services (Form 10-K for 2025, checked 1 October 2026).

## What do the numbers on the table say?

**Who needs the answer.** The finance head, who will check every figure you quote against these
lines.

**The questions on the way.** What does a panel cost Kalpa? How many sell in a normal month, and how
many more does marketing expect?

| What | Number | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Whole-body wellness panel, list price | $299 | Kalpa Health price list |
| The price the marketing head proposes for January | $249 | The marketing head |
| Whole-body panels paid for by the patients themselves in a normal month | 150 | Assumption (the finance head's count) |
| What one panel costs Kalpa: the draw, the reagents, the lab's time and the report | $130 | Assumption |
| Extra self-pay panels the marketing head expects in January at $249 | 40 percent more than a normal month | Assumption |

## What is each head counting?

**Who needs the answer.** Your group, which has to weigh two heads who are both partly right.

**The questions on the way.** Which number does each head watch?

- **The marketing head:** "Forty percent more panels is forty percent more patients who know us
  when they need their next test."
- **The finance head:** "Every panel we would have sold in January anyway now earns $50 less."

## What do you owe Dr Menon when time is called, and how is each of you scored?

**Who needs the answer.** Each of you: Dr Menon acts on one position, and the panel scores every
learner alone on what they said and did in the eighteen minutes.

**The questions on the way.** What does the group hand over? How does the round run? What earns
the marks?

When time is called, the group gives Dr Menon one position, or the point where it splits, named out
loud; the one number that carries the position, and where on this card that number comes from; and
the fact that would change the group's mind, with the main risk of its position.

| Part | Minutes | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Read | 3 | Each of you reads the card alone and may write. |
| Discuss | 18 | The four of you talk to each other. The chair answers no question about the numbers, so state any assumption out loud. |
| The panel's questions | 4 | The chair puts two questions, each to a learner by name. |

You have pen and paper and one phone calculator in the middle of the table. A row whose source says
assumption or illustration is yours to challenge: say what you would use instead, and why.

**Group discussion, 30 marks.** Each learner is scored alone.

| Criterion | Marks | What full marks look like |
|---|---|---|
| Structures the problem | 8 | The learner frames the decision and the metric before arguing. |
| Uses evidence | 8 | The learner takes a position and defends it with a number from the exhibit. |
| Engages | 8 | The learner builds on or challenges another member's point and brings a quiet member in. |
| Lands a conclusion | 6 | The discussion ends on a recommendation and its main risk. |
