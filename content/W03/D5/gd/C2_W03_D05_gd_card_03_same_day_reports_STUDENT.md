# Kalpa Health GD card 03: same-day reports in every city

Handed out when the round opens. Three minutes to read, eighteen to reach a position as a group, then the panel puts two questions to you.

## Dr Menon's ask

> "The marketing head wants to advertise same-day reports in all six cities. The clinics' operations head says two of our labs cannot keep that promise without a second shift. Which of them is right, and what should I do?"

## What your group owes Dr Menon when the discussion closes

1. One position the four of you would put to her, or a disagreement you name out loud if you cannot reach one.
2. The one number that carries the position, and where that number comes from.
3. The fact that would change your mind.

## Who else is in the room

- **The marketing head:** "A promise that holds in four cities and not in two is a promise we cannot print."
- **The clinics' operations head:** "Advertise it where we can keep it, or pay for the shifts first."
