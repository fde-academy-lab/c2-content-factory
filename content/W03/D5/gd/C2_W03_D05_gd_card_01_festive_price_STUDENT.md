# Kalpa Health GD card 01: the festive price

Handed out when the round opens. Three minutes to read, eighteen to reach a position as a group, then the panel puts two questions to you.

## Dr Menon's ask

> "The marketing head wants the Whole-body wellness panel at $249 for the festive month, down from $299, and expects a lot more people to book it. Do we do it? Give me the one number that decides it."

## What your group owes Dr Menon when the discussion closes

1. One position the four of you would put to her, or a disagreement you name out loud if you cannot reach one.
2. The one number that carries the position, and where that number comes from.
3. The fact that would change your mind.

## Exhibit

| What | Number | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Whole-body wellness panel, list price | $299 | Kalpa Health test catalogue |
| Diabetes monitoring panel, list price | $149 | Kalpa Health test catalogue |
| Healthy aging panel, list price | $349 | Kalpa Health test catalogue |
| Whole-body wellness panels booked, April to September | 1,685 | Kalpa Health booking tests, panel lines |
| Cost to Kalpa of performing one Whole-body wellness panel (reagents, collection, reporting) | $130 | This prompt's assumption |
| Extra Whole-body panel bookings the marketing head expects at $249 | 40 percent more than a normal month | This prompt's assumption |
| Share of the half year's bookings that falls in one month | one sixth | This prompt's assumption |

Rows marked **this prompt's assumption** are the prompt's own and do not come from Kalpa Health's files. Argue with them if you think they are wrong, and say what you would use instead.

## Who else is in the room

- **The marketing head:** "Forty percent more checkups is forty percent more patients who come back next year."
- **The finance head:** "Every one of the checkups we would have sold anyway now earns $50 less."
