# Kalpa Health GD card 07: pricing a corporate health-check contract

Handed out when the round opens. Three minutes to read, eighteen to reach a position as a group, then the panel puts two questions to you.

## Dr Menon's ask

> "An IT company wants annual health checks for 2,000 employees and is asking for $110 a check against our list price of $150. The marketing head wants the logo. The finance head wants the money up front. The patient service centres' operations head says the lab cannot take 2,000 checks in a fortnight. Price it, and tell me the terms."

## What your group owes Dr Menon when the discussion closes

1. One position the four of you would put to her, or a disagreement you name out loud if you cannot reach one.
2. The one number that carries the position, and where that number comes from.
3. The fact that would change your mind.

## Exhibit

| Family members the marketing head expects to buy a Whole-body wellness panel at list price within the year | 300 | This prompt's assumption (the marketing head) |

Rows marked **this prompt's assumption** are the prompt's own and do not come from Kalpa Health's files. Argue with them if you think they are wrong, and say what you would use instead.

## Who else is in the room

- **The marketing head:** "The families alone bring in ninety thousand dollars. The logo opens three more companies."
- **The finance head:** "At $110 and ninety days we are lending them money to test their staff."
- **The patient service centres' operations head:** "Two hundred checks a day for two weeks means every retail patient in that city waits."
