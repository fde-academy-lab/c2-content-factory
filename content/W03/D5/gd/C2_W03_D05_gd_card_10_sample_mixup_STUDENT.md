# Kalpa Health GD card 10: the sample mix-up

Handed out when the round opens. Three minutes to read, eighteen to reach a position as a group, then the panel puts two questions to you.

## Dr Menon's ask

> "Last week a home-collection sample at one of our labs was labelled with the wrong patient's name, and a patient was told they were diabetic when they were not. It is in the local news. The head of lab quality wants a second barcode check on every sample, which adds four hours to report time. The clinics' operations head says that breaks our same-day promise. The marketing head wants to pause home collection in that city for a month. The board meets in three days, and I have to walk in with one decision."

## What your group owes Dr Menon when the discussion closes

1. One position the four of you would put to her, or a disagreement you name out loud if you cannot reach one.
2. The one number that carries the position, and where that number comes from.
3. The fact that would change your mind.

## Exhibit

| What | Number | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Home collection's share of bookings | 13 percent | Kalpa Health bookings, both booking systems |
| Samples the labs handle in a year | 30,000 | This prompt's assumption |
| Mislabelled samples found in an audit of the last 40,000 samples | 2 | This prompt's assumption (the head of lab quality) |
| Mislabel rate with the second check, as the vendor states it | 1 in 200,000 | This prompt's assumption (the head of lab quality) |
| Cost of the second check | Rs 9 a sample | This prompt's assumption |
| Patients who told a survey of 150 that same-day reports are why they chose Kalpa | 30 percent | This prompt's assumption (the clinics' operations head) |
| Bookings in that city in a month, and the average value of one | 600, and Rs 1,500 | This prompt's assumption |

Rows marked **this prompt's assumption** are the prompt's own and do not come from Kalpa Health's files. Argue with them if you think they are wrong, and say what you would use instead.

## Who else is in the room

- **The head of lab quality:** "One in twenty thousand sounds small until it is your mother's report."
- **The clinics' operations head:** "Four hours on every sample, for two errors in forty thousand."
- **The marketing head:** "If the story runs again next week, the city is gone for a year, not a month."
