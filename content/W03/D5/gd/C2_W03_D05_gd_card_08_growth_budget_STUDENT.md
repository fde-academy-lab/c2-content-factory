# Kalpa Health GD card 08: where the next $2 million goes

Handed out when the round opens. Three minutes to read, eighteen to reach a position as a group, then the panel puts two questions to you.

## Dr Menon's ask

> "The board has approved $2 million for growth next year, and I can back one plan. The finance head wants a seventh city. The patient service centres' operations head wants a second lab in Dallas. The marketing head wants an at-home collection fleet across all six cities. Each of them has sent me a number. Which plan do I back?"

## What your group owes Dr Menon when the discussion closes

1. One position the four of you would put to her, or a disagreement you name out loud if you cannot reach one.
2. The one number that carries the position, and where that number comes from.
3. The fact that would change your mind.

Rows marked **this prompt's assumption** are the prompt's own and do not come from Kalpa Health's files. Argue with them if you think they are wrong, and say what you would use instead.

## Who else is in the room

- **The finance head:** "A city is an asset. A survey is an opinion."
- **The patient service centres' operations head:** "Dallas is our biggest booking city and our slowest reports."
- **The marketing head:** "Twelve percent across six cities beats nine hundred patients in one."
