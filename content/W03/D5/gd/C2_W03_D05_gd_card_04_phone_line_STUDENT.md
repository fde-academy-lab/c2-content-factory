# Kalpa Health GD card 04: closing the phone line

Handed out when the round opens. Three minutes to read, eighteen to reach a position as a group, then the panel puts two questions to you.

## Dr Menon's ask

> "Online booking takes more than a quarter of our bookings now. The patient service centres' operations head wants to close the phone booking line by March and move everyone online. The marketing head says the phone is how our older patients book. Decide it with me."

## What your group owes Dr Menon when the discussion closes

1. One position the four of you would put to her, or a disagreement you name out loud if you cannot reach one.
2. The one number that carries the position, and where that number comes from.
3. The fact that would change your mind.

## Exhibit

| What | Number | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Bookings by channel, April to September, where the channel is recorded | walk-in 45, online 27, phone 15, at-home 13 percent | Kalpa Health bookings, both booking systems |
| Patients aged 65 and over | 25.4 percent of 6,700 | Kalpa Health patients file |
| Bookings in a year | 23,000 | This prompt's assumption |
| Average value of one booking | $150 | This prompt's assumption |
| Cost of running the phone line | $65,000 a year | This prompt's assumption |
| Where phone bookers go if the line closes | half to online booking, a third to walk-in, the rest may leave Kalpa | This prompt's assumption (the marketing head's estimate from a sample of calls) |

Rows marked **this prompt's assumption** are the prompt's own and do not come from Kalpa Health's files. Argue with them if you think they are wrong, and say what you would use instead.

## Who else is in the room

- **The patient service centres' operations head:** "Sixty-five thousand dollars a year for a line that could be a button."
- **The marketing head:** "The people on that line are the ones who book a Healthy aging panel for both parents."
