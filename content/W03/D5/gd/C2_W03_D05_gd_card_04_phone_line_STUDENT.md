# GD card 04: Should Kalpa Health close its phone booking line and move every patient to online booking?

Build 1, Friday's group discussion. Read the card alone in three minutes; the rules and the scoring
are at its end. Kalpa Health and its people are fictional, and the fact about a real company was
checked on 1 October 2026.

> "Online booking keeps growing. The centres' operations head wants to close the phone line by
> March and send everyone online. The marketing head says the phone is how our older patients book.
> Decide it with me."
> Dr Priya Menon, COO, Kalpa Health

**Who needs the answer.** Dr Menon, who has to tell the phone team this month whether they have a
job in April. Closing the line saves $65,000 a year, and every patient who leaves with it takes
their bookings to another lab.

**The questions on the way.** How do patients book? How many bookings ride on the line, and how many
might leave? How sure is that estimate? What does each head want? What do you owe Dr Menon when
time is called?

## How do patients book, and why is the phone line in question?

**Who needs the answer.** The patient service centres' operations head, who runs the twelve centres
where Kalpa draws blood, and the phone team.

**The questions on the way.** Which ways can a patient book? Does a real lab book patients online
at scale?

A patient books a visit online, by phone, or by coming to a centre without booking. The operations
head wants to close the phone line and point callers to online booking. Quest Diagnostics' patient
portal had more than 45 million registered users at the end of 2025 and lets patients schedule
appointments and receive appointment reminders (Form 10-K for 2025, checked 1 October 2026).

## What do the numbers on the table say?

**Who needs the answer.** The finance head, who will check every figure you quote against these
lines.

**The questions on the way.** How many bookings come by phone? What does a booking bring in? Where
would phone bookers go, and on whose estimate?

| What | Number | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Patients on Kalpa Health's register | 6,700 | Kalpa Health patient register |
| Patients aged 65 and over | 1,699, which is 25.4 percent | Kalpa Health patient register |
| Bookings in a year | 23,000 | Assumption (the operations head's count) |
| Share of bookings made by phone | 15 percent | Assumption (the operations head's count) |
| Cost of running the phone line | $65,000 a year | Assumption |
| What the plans and the patient pay Kalpa for one booking, on average | $85 | Assumption (the finance head) |
| Where phone bookers say they would go if the line closed | half online, a third to a centre without booking, the rest, one in six, to another lab | Assumption (the marketing head, from 60 calls the team listened to) |

## What does each head want?

**Who needs the answer.** Your group, which has to weigh a saving the finance head can count against
a loss the marketing head can only estimate.

**The questions on the way.** Whose estimate is each number?

- **The operations head:** "Sixty-five thousand dollars a year for a line that could be a button
  on our website."
- **The marketing head:** "The people on that line are the ones who book a Healthy aging panel for
  both their parents."

## What do you owe Dr Menon when time is called, and how is each of you scored?

**Who needs the answer.** Each of you: Dr Menon acts on one position, and the panel scores every
learner alone on what they said and did in the eighteen minutes.

**The questions on the way.** What does the group hand over? How does the round run? What earns
the marks?

When time is called, the group gives Dr Menon one position, or the point where it splits, named out
loud; the one number that carries the position, and where on this card that number comes from; and
the fact that would change the group's mind, with the main risk of its position.

| Part | Minutes | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Read | 3 | Each of you reads the card alone and may write. |
| Discuss | 18 | The four of you talk to each other. The chair answers no question about the numbers, so state any assumption out loud. |
| The panel's questions | 4 | The chair puts two questions, each to a learner by name. |

You have pen and paper and one phone calculator in the middle of the table. A row whose source says
assumption or illustration is yours to challenge: say what you would use instead, and why.

**Group discussion, 30 marks.** Each learner is scored alone.

| Criterion | Marks | What full marks look like |
|---|---|---|
| Structures the problem | 8 | The learner frames the decision and the metric before arguing. |
| Uses evidence | 8 | The learner takes a position and defends it with a number from the exhibit. |
| Engages | 8 | The learner builds on or challenges another member's point and brings a quiet member in. |
| Lands a conclusion | 6 | The discussion ends on a recommendation and its main risk. |
