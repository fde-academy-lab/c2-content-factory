    03 · Experiment cards

    What does one change do to one number, experiment by experiment?

    Read the situation and the hypothesis, decide what you expect, then run. Each card says whether its numbers are Kalpa's or invented.

    Experiment A · Kalpa

    What happens when the refresh runs a week later on the same data?

    Situation. The table is built on data that ends 28 September. The refresh runs on 19 October, then again on 26 October, with no new data loaded in between.

    Hypothesis. With recency counted to the run day, the 60-day list grows every week though nobody bought anything less.

    What happened.

    Count recency to the data's last date, and write that date into the table.

    What happened appears here after the run.

    Experiment B · invented feed

    What does a merge with no validate do when every reached row arrives twice?

    Situation. The sale reached three of the four invented customers, and the platform sends each of their rows a second time. The merge runs with no validate argument.

    Hypothesis. Reached spend doubles exactly, and nothing errors.

    What happened.

    Count the rows before and after every merge, and let validate= say it loudly.

    What happened appears here after the run.

    Experiment C · Kalpa

    Can the default mean report Retail-Core moving the wrong way?

    Situation. Kavya asks whether the Retail-Core segment grew from Q1 to Q2. The pivot leaves out aggfunc.

    Hypothesis. The averaged pivot can shrink the change, and it can also report the wrong direction.

    What happened.

    Write aggfunc every time, and check the grand total against the orders.

    What happened appears here after the run.

    Experiment D · Kalpa

    What happens to reached customers whose segment is read from their orders?

    Situation. The reach table takes each reached customer's segment from their orders. groupby runs with its default.

    Hypothesis. Reached customers who never ordered lose their segment and vanish, so the share who bought reads perfect.

    What happened.

    Take the segment from the customer list, and check that the groups add back to the rows.

    What happened appears here after the run.

    Experiment E · Kalpa, then scaled

    Does the tool choice change when the orders table grows a thousand times?

    Situation. Finance's Monday revenue is eight numbers, four segments by two quarters. SQL, pandas and plain Python all return the same eight.

    Hypothesis. SQL moves eight rows whatever the size of the orders table, while pandas moves every order and every customer.

    What happened.

    Size a route by the rows it moves and by who must rerun it.

    04 · Glossary

    What do the day's words mean, and where did each first matter?

    Grain
    What one row of a table is: here, one customer on the list.The morning's opening; chapter 1.
    Spine
    The table every other source is attached to, here the customer list, so no customer can go missing.Chapter 1, notebook 01, question 4.
    Split, apply, combine
    What groupby does: split rows by a key, apply a calculation to each group, combine one result per key.Chapter 1, notebook 01, question 3.
    RFM
    Recency, frequency and monetary value: when a customer last bought, how often, and how much.Chapter 1.
    Fan-out
    A merge that multiplies rows because a key repeats on one side.Chapter 2, notebook 02, question 3.
    validate=
    The merge argument that raises MergeError when the keys break the stated promise.Chapter 2, notebook 02, question 4.
    First touch
    The rule that keeps one exposure per customer, the earliest.Chapter 2, notebook 02, question 5.
    aggfunc
    What pivot_table does with several values in one cell; the default is the mean.Chapter 3, notebook 03, question 3.
    Wide and long
    A column per month to compare along a row, or a row per month to follow a trend.Chapter 3, notebook 03, question 5.
    dropna
    groupby's argument that decides whether a missing key forms a group; the default drops it, where SQL's GROUP BY keeps a NULL group.Chapter 4, notebook 04, question 4.
    Rows moved
    The rows a route pulls out of the warehouse to produce its answer, the size that separates SQL from pandas.Chapter 5, notebook 05, question 3.
    As-of date
    The data's last loaded date, which recency is counted to and which the table carries.Chapter 6, notebook 06, question 3.
    Guard
    A check that raises an error so a table that fails it is never written.Chapter 6, notebook 06, question 4.
