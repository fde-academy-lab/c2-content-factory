# Solution: round 3, the front-page number

Answers: 1c 2b 3d 4a 5b 6c 7c

## The idea being tested

A single number is read correctly only with its period, its comparison and its base. Without a
period, Rs 19.84 crore is read as a doubled quarter; without a base, 29.4 percent is read as a
collapse; and a change is measured on the earlier period, or the fall is overstated.

## Where this appears in production

Board packs and investor slides follow the same rule: every headline number carries its period and
its comparison, and a percentage carries its base. Analysts who present to leadership read their own
card aloud to someone outside the team before it ships.
