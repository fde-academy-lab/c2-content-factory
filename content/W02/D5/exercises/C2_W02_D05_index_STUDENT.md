# Which exercise follows each chapter on Friday, and what question does each ask?

> "Monday's growth review deck needs three things I can open on my laptop without a login: the
> revenue tree by segment for both quarters, the top-fifty protect list with a lookup so I can find
> any member by id, and one number on the front page with its trend. Nothing that needs Python. If a
> director changes an assumption in the room, the sheet must recalculate in front of them."
>
> Meera's chief of staff, Kalpa Retail

Every exercise today puts the chief of staff's ask back to you, or a question one of its answers
raised. Meera Raghavan is Kalpa Retail's CEO and runs Monday's growth review; Kavya Nair, the senior
analyst on the team, reviews every line before it reaches the deck. The day climbs one case in six
chapters, from the tree by segment to a workbook a director cannot break silently, and each chapter
has a short set of its own: items 1 and 2 run live in the chapter's last three minutes; the rest are
the practice lab's stretch or tonight's work. Every stem is a question a Kalpa stakeholder would
ask, and more than a third of the day's items ask you to design the answer before you judge one: the
best-fit approach with a sizing you compute, the fact that would switch the choice, or a second
route that could disagree with the first.

**Who needs the answer.** You, planning the day. Each file below answers one rung of the chief of
staff's ask, and a rung skipped is a part of Monday's file you will build without having practised it.

**The questions on the way.**

- Which file asks which question, and when do you take it?
- What goes in the practice lab, and what is its stretch?
- Where are the answers, and when are they released?

## What goes in the practice lab, and what is its stretch?

The lab's core is its four problems, about 60 minutes: problem 1 fifteen minutes, problem 2 twelve,
problem 3 fifteen and problem 4 twenty. Its stretch is the chapter sets' remaining 24 items, items 3
onward of each set, chapters 4 to 6 first; whatever is left is tonight's work.

## Where are the answers, and when are they released?

lab, the two case solutions after the debrief, with their executed notebooks, and the lab solution at

why it holds and why each other letter fails.
