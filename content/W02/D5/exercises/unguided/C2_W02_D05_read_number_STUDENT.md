# Unguided: read the number

Three figures as they would appear on a front page. Each is missing one of the three things a
number needs, and a director will supply the missing piece from their imagination.

## Q1.

> **Revenue: Rs 9.84 crore**

What is missing? Which one appears?

a) The comparison, so nobody knows if it moved
b) All three: of what, over when, against what
c) The denominator, so nobody knows of what
d) The period, so nobody knows over what time

## Q2.

> **Q2 revenue: Rs 9.84 crore, down 1.6 percent**

What is still missing? Which one appears?

a) Nothing: it has a period and a comparison
b) The period, which is only named as Q2
c) The comparison, since down needs a baseline
d) The denominator: across how many orders

## Q3.

> **Retail-Plus frequency: down 34.9 percent against 462 orders in the quarter**

What is wrong here? Which one appears?

a) Nothing at all: all three pieces are present
b) The comparison is missing from it entirely
c) The denominator belongs to another number
d) The period is stated twice and they conflict

## Q4.

A director reads a falling trend line with no sentence beside it. Which one appears?

a) They will ask you what caused it before acting
b) They will supply their own reason for the fall
c) They will treat a chart without words as noise
d) They will wait for the next quarter to confirm

## Answering

Post one line: the four letters in order. Then rewrite Q1 as a single sentence that a director
could repeat in a meeting you are not in, without the meaning changing.
