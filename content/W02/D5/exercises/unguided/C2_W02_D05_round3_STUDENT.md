# Round 3: the front-page number

Ten minutes, alone, at the end of round 3. Seven items on the card a director reads in two minutes.
The solution opens at the close of the session.

Post exactly this shape, the letters in item order, no spaces: `xxxxxxx`

---

### Q1. The draft card reads "Revenue Rs 19.84 crore". A director remembers Rs 10.00 crore for Q1. What does the card lack that lets them read it as a doubled quarter?

a) Its denominator, the company's revenue in the same period
b) Its trend, the monthly line beside the number
c) Its period, the months the number covers
d) Its segment split, the tree underneath it

### Q2. The second slot reads "Retail-Plus revenue down 29.4 percent". What makes it readable by a director who has not seen the tree?

a) Rounding it to 29 percent so it reads as an estimate
b) The rupee base and the segment's share of the quarter
c) A red arrow so the direction cannot be missed
d) Comparing it with last year's fall in the same months

### Q3. Retail-Plus revenue was Rs 5,85,770 in Q1 and Rs 4,13,380 in Q2. What is the change the card should print?

a) Down 41.7 percent
b) Down 17.3 percent
c) Down 1.6 percent
d) Down 29.4 percent

### Q4. On the company line, July's revenue is 45 percent above June's. What is the honest reading for the front page?

c) July is the best month of the half-year for growth

a) The formula it used for the change
