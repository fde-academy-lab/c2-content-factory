# What must sit beside the front-page number so a director reads it right in two minutes?

Chapter 4 set, six items, after chapter 4: items 1 and 2 run live in the chapter's last three
minutes; the rest are the practice lab's stretch or tonight's work.

> "... and one number on the front page with its trend."
>
> Meera's chief of staff, Kalpa Retail

The front page of Monday's growth review deck carries one number, revenue in Q2 against Q1, and
Meera Raghavan, Kalpa Retail's CEO, and her directors read it first and may read nothing else. Q1 runs
from April to June 2026 and Q2 from July to September 2026. The warehouse is the database that holds
one row per order and that every sheet ties back to, and Finance, Kalpa's finance team, owns the
booked revenue it holds. Counted once per order and tied to the warehouse, revenue was Rs 10,00,00,000
in Q1 and Rs 9,84,00,000 in Q2. A card is the number with what it needs to be read right: its period
(the months it covers), its comparison (the figure it is set against), its base (the rupees a
percentage is taken of, and the number's share of the whole), and a sentence on what moved it. A
change is measured on the earlier period: Q2 minus Q1, divided by Q1. Beneath the company's card,
the front page has a second slot, which carries one segment's card. Kalpa's segments are
Retail-Core (everyday shoppers), Retail-Plus (the paid membership tier), Business (corporate buyers
invoiced in large amounts) and Student.

**Who needs the answer.** Meera and the directors read the front page in about two minutes, and the
chief of staff presents it and answers for it. A card without its period reads two quarters as one,
and a percentage without its base turns a small fall into a crisis the meeting spends its time on.

**The questions on the way.**

- What does a card that reads "Revenue Rs 19.84 crore" lack?
- What change does the Retail-Plus line print, and what sits beside it?
- Which line reports a share that moved from 20 to 25 percent?
- Which card answers a director who asks for the number without Business?
- What is the honest reading of July's jump on the company line?
- Which comparison does September's card carry when the board reviews each month against its plan?

Item 3's channel and item 6's plan are invented; every other number is Kalpa's own.

### Q1. What does a card that reads "Revenue Rs 19.84 crore" lack?

The fastest draft of the card is the export's grand total in big type, "Revenue Rs 19.84 crore",
which is Kalpa's revenue for April to September together. A director remembers Rs 10.00 crore for
Q1. What does the card lack that lets the director read a quarter that nearly doubled?

a) Its base, the company's revenue in the same months
b) Its trend, the monthly line drawn beside the number
c) Its period, the months the number covers
d) Its segment split, the tree on the next page

### Q2. What change does the Retail-Plus line print, and what sits beside it?

Retail-Plus booked Rs 5,85,770 in Q1 and Rs 4,13,380 in Q2, and the company booked Rs 9,84,00,000 in
Q2. The front page's second slot carries Retail-Plus. What change does its card print, and what sits
beside it?

a) Down 41.7 percent, beside Rs 4.13 lakh, Rs 5.86 lakh and 0.4 percent
b) Down 29.4 percent, beside Rs 4.13 lakh, Rs 5.86 lakh and 0.4 percent
c) Down 29.4 percent alone, since a percentage carries its own base with it
d) Down Rs 1.72 lakh alone, since a rupee figure needs no base beside it

### Q3. Which line reports a share that moved from 20 to 25 percent?

An invented app channel carried 20 percent of a business's revenue in Q1 and 25 percent in Q2.
Which line goes on the card?

a) App share up 5 percent on Q1, to 25 percent of revenue
b) App share up 5 points on Q1, to 25 percent of revenue
c) App share up 25 points on Q1, to 25 percent of revenue
d) App share up 0.25 on Q1, to 25 percent of revenue

### Q4. Which card answers a director who asks for the number without Business?

A director says Business invoices are lumpy, meaning a few large invoices make up most of a month and
their timing moves the total, and asks for the front page without them. Without Business, the three
consumer segments booked Rs 9.86 lakh in Q1 and Rs 8.15 lakh in Q2. Which card answers the director
and stays honest?

a) The company card, replaced by a new line: Q2 revenue down 17.3 percent on Q1
b) The company card left as it is, since Business is part of the revenue Finance signs
c) A second card, headed All except Business: Rs 8.15 lakh, down 17.3 percent, 0.8 percent of company revenue
d) A second card, headed All except Business: Rs 8.15 lakh, down 17.3 percent, 0.4 percent of company revenue

### Q5. What is the honest reading of July's jump on the company line?

The company's revenue runs Rs 3.12 crore in June and Rs 4.51 crore in July, 45 percent higher, then
Rs 2.69 crore in August and Rs 2.64 crore in September. Without Business, the line runs Rs 3.32 lakh in
June and slides to Rs 2.48 lakh by September. What is the honest reading for the front page?

a) A monsoon sale lifted every segment in July, and the lift faded by August
b) July was the half-year's best month for growth across the whole business
c) The rise is seasonal, and it will come back next July without any action
d) Business invoices landed in July, and the consumer line fell all quarter

### Q6. Which comparison does September's card carry when the board reviews each month against its plan?

The board decides to review the business every month against its plan line, the revenue the year's
plan set for each month. September's revenue was Rs 2,64,40,430, after Rs 2,69,02,240 in August, and
September's plan, an invented figure, is Rs 3,00,00,000. Which comparison does September's card
carry, and what does it print?

a) September against its plan: Rs 2.64 crore, 11.9 percent below the plan's Rs 3.00 crore
b) September against August: Rs 2.64 crore, down 1.7 percent on August's Rs 2.69 crore
c) Q2 against Q1: Rs 9.84 crore, down 1.6 percent, since the deck already prints it
d) September against its plan: Rs 2.64 crore, 13.5 percent below the plan's Rs 3.00 crore
