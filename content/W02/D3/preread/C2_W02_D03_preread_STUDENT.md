# Before Wednesday: one question with three right answers

Ten minutes.

## The thing to think about

Eight members, ranked by what they spent:

| member | spend |
|---|---|
| A | Rs 9,400 |
| B | Rs 8,100 |
| C | Rs 7,250 |
| D | Rs 6,600 |
| E | Rs 6,600 |
| F | Rs 5,900 |

Somebody asks for "the top five". Write down how many names you would send, and which.

D and E spent the same. Decide what you would do about that before tomorrow, and write your
reasoning in one line. Tomorrow the head of Retail-Plus will tell you what he wants, and you will
find out whether your instinct matched a real stakeholder's.

## Three words

| Word | What it means here |
|---|---|
| Window | A set of rows related to the current row, over which a function is computed |
| Partition | Which rows count as related, set by `PARTITION BY` |
| Rank | A row's position within its window, under one of three rules |
