Where does the line go?

The same model, two businesses. Slide the line, change the price ratio, and watch the four boxes and the rupee bill redraw. Real scores: the bank's 8,238 validation clients, and German Credit's 300 held-out applicants.

  1. Lock a guess for the cheapest line, then read the opening state aloud: the line at 0.5, the four boxes, the bill.
  2. Drag the line left and watch the bill fall, then press reveal and compare it with your guess.
  3. Move the ratio from 30 to 1 towards 8 to 1, or tick the budget, and watch the model's edge over the no-model rule grow; then switch to lending.
