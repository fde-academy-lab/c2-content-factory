# Week 5: the machine learning core

One public dataset carries the whole week: a Portuguese bank's phone campaign (UCI Bank Marketing,
41,188 calls, CC BY 4.0), downloaded in code by every notebook. Kalpa is the business the
decisions are made for: Marketing's Rs 40 nudge on Monday to Thursday, and Rohan's lending book
on Tuesday and Friday, which Build 2 picks up next week.

## One picture grows all week

Each deck opens on the same timeline and adds a piece: Monday the past rows, the cut, the
prediction moment and the outcome window; Tuesday the two score humps, the line and the four boxes;
Wednesday the column written after the prediction moment, in red; Thursday the U of validation
error against depth; Friday the score sagging from 2008 to 2010. Redraw it from memory each night.

## The sealed envelope

On Monday a card marked TEST SET, DO NOT OPEN UNTIL FRIDAY goes on the wall. Every request to peek
at the test score is pointed at it. On Friday it is opened once, the chosen model is scored once,
and that number goes into the committee memo.

ME1 runs this week as a continuous activity; its scope is statistics, data manipulation, analyst
technique and the ML core. The IITGN faculty blocks W5-1 to W5-5 are tentative.
