Press reshuffle five times: one split's number jumps; the mean of five holds.

Three clicks
  1. Read the two numbers at 12 bends with the brake off. Name what you see.
  2. Slide the bends down to 3 or 4. Validation error falls to its floor while training error rises a little; the strip draws a U. The gap is variance; the floor is bias.
  3. Go back to 12 bends and raise the brake to 0.001. Validation lands near the 3-bend value: the brake is a second road to the same place. Push it to 3 and both errors climb: too much brake is bias.
