# Guesstimate of the day: how many two-wheelers are sold in India in a year?

**TRAINER ONLY.** Five minutes, in the interview room slot (half two, S9) or as the lab's warm-up.
The room estimates first; the anchor is revealed last.

## How does a strong candidate run it?

| Step | What they say | The numbers |
|---|---|---|
| Clarify | "New vehicles sold to buyers in India in one financial year, scooters and motorcycles together, exports left out." | |
| Map | "Households, times the share that own a two-wheeler, gives the fleet a year needs to keep; over the years before a household replaces one, that is the replacement demand; then add first-time buyers on top." | |
| Assume, with ranges | "30 crore households, from 28 to 32 crore; half own one, from 40 to 60 percent; replaced every 8 years, from 6 to 10; first-time buyers add 15 percent, from 10 to 20." | Every input is an assumption, said as one |
| Calculate | "30 crore x 0.5 / 8 is about 1.9 crore a year of replacements; times 1.15 is about 2.16 crore." | About 1.2 crore to about 3.8 crore across the ranges, every extreme multiplied together, so the years before replacement is the input to argue about |
| Validate | "SIAM reported 2,17,05,974 domestic two-wheeler sales in FY 2025-26, so I am within 1 percent, which is luck as much as method." | SIAM's release for FY 2025-26 (T3, checked 12 October 2026) |
| The catch | "October 2025's retail alone was 31,49,846, so a festive month times twelve gives about 3.78 crore, 74 percent too high. A seasonal series is never annualised from its peak month." | FADA's release of 7 November 2025 (F5, checked 12 October 2026) |

## What does each wrong turn look like, and what do you say?

| The wrong turn | What to say |
|---|---|
| Starts from India's population and divides by family size, then stops | "How many of those households own one, and how often do they buy?" |
| Annualises a month's news headline | "Which month was that, and does every month sell like it?" |
| Gives one number with no range | "Which input are you least sure of, and what happens to the answer at its ends?" |
| Forgets first-time buyers | "Is every sale a replacement?" |

## Where is the anchor from?

SIAM, "Auto Industry Performance of Q4 (Jan- March 2026) & FY 2025-26", which SIAM's site resolves
by its trailing number, reports 2,17,05,974 domestic two-wheeler sales in FY 2025-26, up 10.7 percent:
https://www.siam.in/news-&-updates/press-releases/x/605 (checked 12 October 2026).

FADA's October 2025 retail figure is from its release of 7 November 2025:
https://fada.in/images/press-release/1690d68d7a2e6cFADA%20releases%20October%202025%20&%2042-Days%20Festive%20Vehicle%20Retail%20Data.pdf (checked 12 October 2026).

FADA counts retail registrations, which lag sales, and SIAM reports manufacturers' domestic sales, so
the two series differ in timing as well as scope.
