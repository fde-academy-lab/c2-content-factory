## Which item is worth arguing about?

Q2's option b. Team B's 6,000 is the best number on the table, and a room under time pressure takes
it. It was measured on the 91 weeks the model was fitted to, so it is a fit; rescored on the thirteen
hidden weeks it may land anywhere. Team C is the harder call: its 415 is a real lead on these weeks,
and Wednesday's lesson on chance says one origin and thirteen weeks cannot yet tell it from luck.
