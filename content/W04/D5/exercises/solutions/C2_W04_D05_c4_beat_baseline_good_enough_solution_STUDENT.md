## Which item is worth arguing about?

Q2's option b. Team B's 6,000 is the best number on the table, and a room under time pressure takes
it. It was measured on the 91 weeks the model was fitted to, so it is a fit; rescored on the thirteen
hidden weeks it may land anywhere.

## Where does the pattern live at work?

Every model review at a company that has a baseline running, a ranking model at an e-commerce
company or a demand model at a quick-commerce company, asks which data the score came from, whether
the gain clears the baseline by enough to change a decision, and what the decision-maker reads first.
