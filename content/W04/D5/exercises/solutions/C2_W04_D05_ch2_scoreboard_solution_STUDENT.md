## What does the set test?

It tests the scoreboard's three habits: a score is only as good as the weeks behind it, a model must
beat the bot by enough to change a decision, and a price on each direction changes what "best" means.

## Which item is worth arguing about?

Q3's option a. The vendor's misses really are cheaper on these weeks: 12 of its 13 sit too high, and
spare capacity is the cheap mistake. The case against it is what nobody chose: its lean was never
sized, it ran 3.6 percent high in the first six hidden weeks and 11.5 percent in the last six, and in
the festive weeks it copies 2025's calendar. A lean the two costs size is the same idea done on purpose.

## Where does the pattern live at work?

Every forecast review at a company that books capacity ahead, from fulfilment centres to cloud
capacity for an AI launch, asks the same three things: which weeks was it scored on, did it beat the
simple copy, and which way do its misses run.
