# Which exercise answers which question on Friday, and when is each sat?

Friday's case is Flipkart's Lucknow fulfilment centre: the planner must book packer shifts for the
eight festive weeks beginning 12 October to 30 November 2026, between a vendor's forecast 22 percent
above last year's and finance's rule of last year plus 4 percent. Illustrative case: the company is
real, the situation and the data are synthetic, built for this class. The exercises climb the day's
ladder from a hand count (L1) to a design call a hiring panel asks (L5), and the Kalpa set
moves the method to the programme's client.

**Who needs the answer.** You, to know which set to open, when, and what each one practises.

**The questions on the way.**

- Which sets run in the morning, and which in the afternoon?
- Which set moves the method to Kalpa, and when is it sat?
- Where are the answers?

## Which sets run when, and what does each practise?

| When | Set | Items | Levels | What it practises |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chapter 1, with the trainer | `guided/C2_W04_D05_same_week_rule_STUDENT.md` | 3 | L1, L2 | The house bot's rule built by hand on twelve weeks, then hidden and scored: worked, half worked, then yours |
| End of chapter 1, 10 minutes | `unguided/C2_W04_D05_ch1_shapes_STUDENT.md` | 3 | L1, L3 | Trend and season read off three series, a seasonal forecast, and the fix when a series also climbs |
| End of chapter 2, 15 minutes | `unguided/C2_W04_D05_ch2_scoreboard_STUDENT.md` | 3 | L3, L5 | A leaky score found in code, good enough or not on three model scores, and priced misses read for a decision |
| Afternoon, 20 minutes in groups | `unguided/C2_W04_D05_staffing_note_STUDENT.md` | 3 and the note | L4, L5 | The forecast picked, a week's shifts sized, the fact that would move the plan, then the staffing note |
| Lab, after the Kalpa notebook | `unguided/C2_W04_D05_kalpa_board_STUDENT.md` | 3 | L2, L5 | Kalpa's board number: the two scores, the forecast, its error bar |
| Lab, the practice set | `practice/C2_W04_D05_lab_set_STUDENT.md` | 6 | L1 to L5 | The whole method on invented shops and series, ending on a plan share from two costs |

Nine of the 21 items are design calls: the best-fit forecast, a sizing, the fact that would switch
the call, or a second route. The interview level, L6, is practised on the transcripts in `extras/`.

## Where are the answers?

Every set has a solution file in `solutions/`, named after it with `_solution` added, opened after
the set is sat. Each
gives the answer letters, why each key holds and why each other letter fails, and the item worth
arguing about.
