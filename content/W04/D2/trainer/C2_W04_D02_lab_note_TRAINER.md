# Lab note for the TA, Week 4 Tuesday: how do you run the Kalpa transfer and the practice set?

**TRAINER ONLY.** The TA-led practice lab after the afternoon block. It re-runs the day's two moves on
Kalpa Retail: the studio as a three-minute slide to a TA playing one Kalpa head, then the dojo on Anand
Iyer's new-store question, checked against DMart. No plant today. The three Kalpa role cards and
Anand's question are proposed for client zero v2.3 (the row, 21 September 2026, not yet locked).

**Who needs the answer.** The TA running the lab alone.

**The questions on the way.** What runs, in what order? What do learners hold? Where do they stall, and
what is the one hint for each? What does the weak learner do instead?

## What runs, and what do learners hold?

**Who needs the answer.** The TA at the start of the lab. **The questions on the way.** Which files,
which order?

| Order | What runs | Learners hold |

With 35 learners and three minutes each plus one minute of feedback, the presentations need about two
hours with one TA; with two TAs, run two queues of about seventeen each, and let the queue that is not
presenting work on the store question.

## Where do learners stall in the store question, and what is the hint?

**Who needs the answer.** The TA walking the room. **The questions on the way.** Which stall, which
hint?

| The stall | The one hint |
|---|---|
| No idea where to start the map | "Who lives near the store, and how many of them walk in during a year?" |
| A chain with no ranges | "Which of these numbers are you least sure of? Give it a low and a high." |
| A check that compares sign-ups with DMart's revenue | "Turn your shoppers into a year of sales first: visits a month times spend per visit." |
| An estimate above DMart's mature average | "Should a store in its first year outsell a mature store? Which assumption is too high?" |
| The year-end and average store counts mixed up | "Which stores were open for the whole year? Divide by those." |

The worked answer (about 1,000 sign-ups; Rs 72 crore a year, about half a mature DMart store) is in
`trainer/C2_W04_D02_guesstimate_TRAINER.md` and in the lab set's solution.

## What does the weak learner do instead?

**Who needs the answer.** The TA with a learner who cannot start. **The questions on the way.** What is
the entry, and what is the next step?

The Parsons problem at the end of the lab set: four lines of the notebook's second code cell,
scrambled, put in the only order that runs (q s r p) before any line is typed. Then the learner
presents the Kalpa slide with the TA's prompt for each part, one part at a time.

## Where do the practice problems stall?

| Problem | Where learners stall | The one hint |
|---|---|---|
| P1, titles | They rewrite the topic in longer words | "Put a verb in it and say what happens next." |
| P2, the food stall | They pick per order because it is smallest | "What does the scheme pay for? Read in that unit." |
| P3, the four moves | They skip the concession | "What is true in what driver operations said?" |
| P4, what leaves | They cut the guardrails | "Which page would the head who owns it look on?" |
| P5, the combined slide | The estimate appears without its range | "Where does the range go: evidence or caveat?" |
