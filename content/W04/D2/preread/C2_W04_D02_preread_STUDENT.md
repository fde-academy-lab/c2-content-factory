## What do support, confidence and lift each count?

A **basket** is one order with everything in it: one bill at a shop, one cart on an app.

| Word | What it counts, in plain words |
|---|---|
| Support | Of all the baskets, the share that hold both items |
| Confidence | Of the baskets that hold the first item, the share that also hold the second |
| Lift | How many times more often the two items turn up together than they would if shoppers picked each one with no regard for the other |
| Cross-sell | Suggesting a second item to go with the first |
| Upsell | Suggesting a bigger or dearer version of the same item |
| Expected value | What a choice is worth on average, once each outcome is weighed by how often it happens |

The words come with no numbers yet, because Wednesday counts each one by hand before any code runs.

## What is Wednesday's ticket about?

**Swiggy Instamart, Pune, the cart page's "frequently bought together" strip of three slots.** On
Monday 7 September 2026 the strip's ranking changed from a list a person chose to the items most often
found in the same cart as the item a shopper is looking at. The strip's click-through, the share of
times a suggestion shown is tapped, fell from 6.1 percent in the three weeks before the change to 3.8
percent in the three weeks after.
