# Which letters answer the Kalpa store set, and what does the DMart check really say?

Answers: 1c 2b

The case: Anand Iyer, Kalpa Retail's finance controller, wants a rough count of the customers a new
store could sign up to Retail-Plus in its first year. The closest published check is DMart's FY26
release (2 May 2026, checked 12 October 2026): standalone total revenue of Rs 66,968 crore, 500 stores
at 31 March 2026, 85 of them added during the year, so 415 at the start.

## What does the set test?

The denominator decides a per-store figure, and a check passes only when it counts the same thing as
the estimate. DMart's figure is for mature stores, most of them open for years; a store in its first
year sells less, which is why DMart reports its two-year-old stores separately.

## Why is each key right, and why does each other option fail?

| Item | Key | The question in one line | Why the key holds | Why each other option fails |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | c | What is DMart's FY26 revenue per store on the average of its opening and closing counts? | The average of 415 and 500 is 457.5 stores, and Rs 66,968 crore over 457.5 is about Rs 146 crore. | a: Rs 134 crore divides by the year-end 500, counting the 85 new stores as if they sold all year. b: Rs 161 crore divides by the 415 at the start, leaving out the new stores' sales base. d: Rs 788 crore divides by the 85 stores added, the wrong group entirely. |
| 2 | b | What does the validate step say about a first-year Kalpa store selling Rs 150 crore? | A new store sells below a mature chain's average, so an estimate above DMart's about Rs 146 crore points at an assumption that is too high: footfall or spend per visit. | a: sitting near a mature store's average is the warning, since a first-year store should sit well below it. c: a different product range changes the size of the gap, and the check still bounds the estimate. d: older stores' growth of 10.8 percent says nothing about a new store's first year. |

## Which item is worth arguing about?

Item 2. Some will say a large new Kalpa store could outsell an average DMart. It could, in a dense
catchment, and then the estimate has to say why, with its footfall and its spend per visit written
down. An estimate that lands above the check without that sentence has not been validated.

## Where does a first-year check show up at work?

Every new store, branch or dark store a finance team funds: the business case is built on mature
averages and has to be discounted for the ramp. The lab sizes Anand's question this way, and the
Week 5 handover carries the number.
