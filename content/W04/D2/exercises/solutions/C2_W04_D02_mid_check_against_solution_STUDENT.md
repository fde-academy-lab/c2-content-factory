# Which letters answer the drill on the known number each estimate is checked against?

Answers: 1b 2c 3d 4a

The four estimates were UPI payments in September 2026, the trips people make on the Delhi Metro in
a day, a mature grocery store's yearly sales and Uber drivers online at Hyderabad's evening peak. The
published figures were checked on 12 October 2026: NPCI through Outlook Money (802 million UPI
transactions a day in September 2026, 24.07 billion in the month), DMRC through The Tribune
(64.06 lakh journeys a day), DMRC through Republic World (a record single day, 82,44,903 on 17 September
2026), PTI in 2018 (a line-utilisation count of 45,35,932), PTI in 2023 (a journey counts each line a
rider uses) and DMart's FY26 release (Rs 66,968
crore, 500 stores, 85 added).

## What does the drill test?

A check counts only when it sits in the same unit, the same period and the same definition as the
estimate. Each wrong option breaks one of the three: a daily figure for a monthly estimate, a record
day for a typical one, a revenue total with no store count beneath it, a sign-up count for drivers
online at one moment.

## Why is each key right, and why does each other option fail?

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | b | Which check fits UPI payments in the whole of September 2026? | NPCI's 24.07 billion is a count of payments for the same month. | a: 802 million is a per-day figure, another period. c: Rs 29.37 lakh crore is value in rupees, and the estimate counts payments. d: 802 million times 31 builds a month on the wrong number of days, since September has 30, and lands at 24.86 billion. |
| 2 | c | Which check fits the trips people make on the Delhi Metro on a typical day this year? | DMRC's 64.06 lakh is an average day of the same year, so it matches the period and the kind of day. ## Which item is worth arguing about?

Item 3, option b. Some will pick the 415 stores open at the start, since those stores sold all year.
It leaves out the 85 stores that opened and sold for part of the year, so it overstates a store, about
Rs 161 crore. The year-end count, 500, understates for the opposite reason, about Rs 134 crore. The
average of the two counts, about Rs 146 crore, sits between and is the check for a mature store; any of
them is too high for a store in its first year, which is the catch in the lab's store question.

## Where does checking in the same unit show up at work?

Checking in the same unit comes up in every guesstimate round and in every review where someone says "that matches the published
figure". The first question back is "in which unit, for which period, counted how?"
