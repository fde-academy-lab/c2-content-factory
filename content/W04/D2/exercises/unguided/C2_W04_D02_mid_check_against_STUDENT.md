# Which known number would you check each of four estimates against?

An estimate is only as good as its sanity check, and a check only counts when it sits in the same
unit, the same period and the same definition as the estimate. A team at a consulting firm has four
estimates on its desk from last month's interview practice, and for each one it has to name the
published number it will check against. Every published figure below was checked on 12 October 2026.

**Who needs the answer.** The interviewer, who scores the sanity check more than the final number,
and anyone who will act on an estimate before the real data arrives.

**The questions on the way.**

- Which check fits UPI payments a day?
- Which check fits Delhi Metro journeys a day?
- Which check fits a mature grocery store's yearly sales?
- Which check fits drivers online at Hyderabad's evening peak?

## Which check fits each estimate?

This comes up in every guesstimate round: the interviewer asks "how would you check that?" before
asking for the number.

### Q1. Design. The estimate is UPI payments in India on an average day in September 2026. Which check fits?

*Level L5, a design call · asked in consulting and fintech screens.*

a) NPCI's September 2026 total of 24.07 billion transactions, divided by 31 days
b) NPCI's average of 802 million transactions a day in September 2026
c) The value of UPI payments in September 2026, Rs 29.37 lakh crore
d) A published count of the people in India who own a smartphone

### Q2. Design. The estimate is passenger journeys on the Delhi Metro on an average day in 2026. Which check fits?

*Level L5, a design call · asked in GCC and consulting screens.*

a) DMRC's record of 82,44,903 passenger boardings on 17 September 2026
b) DMRC's 2018 line-utilisation count of 45,35,932 for one day
c) DMRC's average of 64.06 lakh passenger journeys a day in 2026
d) A count of commuters taken at the gates of three busy stations

### Q3. Design. The estimate is what one mature large grocery store sells in a year. Which check fits?

*Level L5, a design call · asked in retail and consulting screens.*

a) DMart's FY26 standalone total revenue, Rs 66,968 crore
b) DMart's revenue divided by the 415 stores open at the start of the year
c) DMart's 10.8 percent growth in stores two years and older
d) DMart's revenue divided by the average of 415 and 500 stores

### Q4. Design. The estimate is Uber drivers online at one weekday evening peak in Hyderabad. Which check fits?

*Level L5, a design call · asked in product-company and AI-startup screens.*

a) The case data's online driver-hours behind each rider request, with the city count to be found
b) A published count of Uber drivers signed up in Hyderabad
c) Monday's 256,556 rider requests across the twelve zones in the six weeks that followed the launch
d) The 1,200 drivers in Monday's driver table, counted as the city's supply
