## Where does an element go when no claim needs it and nobody will ask?

This comes up at work whenever a draft is cut down before a review: an element earns its place by the
claim or by a named reader's question.

Ramesh's first draft (invented) carries eight elements, among them the rain days: 2 in September and
2 in October, so the weather dragged both months alike. The claim does not need them, and neither his
son nor the accountant will ask about them.

### Q1. Where do the rain days go?

*Level L1 · the chapter's closing check.*

a) Onto the slide, beside the chart, since rain moves a shop's sales
b) The son's page, since he worked the counter on rainy days
c) Nowhere: they leave, since no claim needs them and nobody will ask
d) The accountant's page, since every number the shop counts belongs in its accounts somewhere

## What happens to the caveat on a crowded slide?

This comes up at work when a slide has room for one more element and the caveat looks like the easiest
thing to move.

### Q2. Design. The slide is crowded, and someone suggests moving the festival fortnight, the caveat, to the appendix. What do you do?

*Level L3, a design call · tagged [F] in this programme's calibration: frequent in global capability centre
(GCC) and product-company screens.*

a) Move it, since a caveat is a detail the owner can find behind the slide
b) Keep it on the slide and move another element off instead
c) Drop it, since the son will not raise it if it is not shown
d) Move it to the speaker's notes, so it is said aloud and never shown

## How is an appendix page headed?

This comes up at work the moment a role asks a question in the meeting: the analyst turns to the page
headed by it.

### Q3. Design. The accountant will ask about the helper's wage and the four weeks' totals. How should her appendix page be headed?

*Level L3, a design call · tagged [F].*

a) What did the longer hours cost, and what did they bring?
b) Appendix 2
c) Costs and totals
d) The helper's wage at Rs 85 an hour, the two extra hours a day, and both months' totals in lakh

---

**What does the chapter add?** An element stays on the slide only if the claim needs it; one a named
person will ask about goes to that person's page, headed by their question; the rest leaves; and the
caveat stays whatever else happens. Chapter 5 builds one appendix page for each of Uber's three roles.
