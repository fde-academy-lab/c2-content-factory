# Can you write the redesign as one page the city head reads in two minutes?

Uber, Hyderabad, the peak-hour earnings guarantee. Since the launch on 31 August 2026, peak online
hours rose 24 percent while completed peak trips rose 3 percent, so gross bookings per peak online hour
fell from Rs 314 to Rs 260 at a fare that held at Rs 165. Rs 1.16 crore of the Rs 1.9 crore top-up
went to driver-weeks that accepted fewer than half their offers. The redesign pays per completed peak
trip with an acceptance floor. The monsoon ended inside the six weeks after the launch, and last
year's weeks are not in the data.

> Illustrative case: the company is real, the situation and the data are synthetic, built for this class.

**Who needs the answer.** The Hyderabad city head, who will read your page before Thursday's city
review, with the driver-operations lead, the marketing lead and the finance partner reading over a
shoulder.

**The questions on the way.**

- What is the claim, in the first sentence?
- Which one number, with its denominator, carries it?
- Where does the caveat sit?
- What is the action, what does it cost, and who signs?

---

## What are the rules?

This is AI-free individual work: forty minutes, on one sheet, by hand, handed in at the end of the
slot. No laptop, no phone, no notes beyond this brief.

---

## What must the page do?

This comes up at work whenever a decision-maker asks for "a page on it" instead of a meeting.

| Order | What it carries |
|---|---|
| First sentence | The claim, in words a driver would follow |
| Second | One number with its denominator: rupees per peak online hour, before and after |
| In the body | The caveat, in a full sentence, never in a bracket at the end |
| Last | The action, its saving in rupees over a stated number of weeks with the assumption behind it, and who signs |

Keep words a non-technical reader would not know off the page: denominator, ratio of sums,
regression. Say what they mean instead.

---

## What does the checklist ask when the page comes back?

| Question | Yes or no |
|---|---|
| Is the claim the first sentence? | |
| Is the number given with its denominator? | |
| Is the caveat in the body of the page? | |
| Is the action given with its cost and who signs? | |
| Is the page free of method words a non-technical reader would not know? | |

Three or more yes answers is a page the city head can use.
