# Week 4 Saturday: the mock-interview round, run sheet

**TRAINER ONLY.** 30 minutes in pairs, run by the Academic TA after the transfer drill. Every learner
holds `C2_W04_SAT_mock_card_STUDENT.md`: the week's transcript questions and their AI-company
variants, the follow-ups to choose from, and the seven-line scorecard, each line 0 to 2. The
scorecard is the spine's own; its scope, structure and recommendation lines echo what Aced's guide
says interviews test, structured thinking and a clear recommendation (checked 13 October 2026), and
the other lines are the programme's. The full hire-grade answers sit in the five transcripts, one per
day, in each day's `extras` folder.

---

## The shape, 30 minutes

| Part | Duration | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Pairing | 2 min | Pair learners who sat apart in the paper and at different drill tables, so neither has rehearsed the other's brief. With an odd room, one trio rotates: A asks B, B asks C and C asks A, at 8 minutes a turn. |
| Round one | 12 min | The interviewer picks one question and one follow-up from the card, asks the question, waits, then asks the follow-up. The candidate answers aloud with no paper. The interviewer scores the seven lines and gives one sentence: the line that scored lowest and what a 2 would have said. |
| Round two | 12 min | They swap seats. The question comes from a different day, and at least one of the pair's two questions is an AI-company variant. |
| Back to the room | 4 min | Two pairs say which line scored lowest. Ask that pair's question to a third person, cold, and let the room score it aloud on that one line. |

Walk the room for both rounds. Sit in on pairs that have gone quiet, and on any pair whose
interviewer is reading the answer off the transcript; the card carries the questions so the
interviewer listens instead. A pair that finishes early takes the stretch page's follow-ups from the
paper, which are the kind an interviewer asks after a first answer.

## What do you listen for in every answer?

Three things, in this order, the same as the week's interview rooms: the answer starts with the
structure and the business consequence, it names a check with a number, and it stops when the
question is answered. A long answer that lists every number in the case scores lower on structure
than a short one that carries four numbers it can explain.

The AI variants score lower on sizing and on cost by design, since they carry no company data; say so
when a pair asks why a strong answer to a variant scored below 14.

## What happens to the scores?

Nothing leaves the pair. The total out of 14 is for the pair's own talk, and no card is collected or
read out by name. Note on a slip the two lines the room scored lowest across the pairs you sat with;
they go with the paper's tag tally to the first session of Week 5.
