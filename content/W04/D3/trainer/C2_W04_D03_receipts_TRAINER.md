# Run sheet: receipts on the table, from ten dots to the action-map sort

**TRAINER ONLY.** This sheet runs the day's signature activity: the room counts support, confidence
and lift by hand on ten tea-stall orders and twelve real carts before any code opens, then sorts
pair cards on the wall's action map in chapter 3. A trainer who did not write the day can run it
from this page alone.

## Who plays which role?

| Role | Who | What they do |
|---|---|---|
| Trainer | The day's trainer | Runs the board, says the lines below, writes every count the room calls out before any formula is named |
| TA | The day's TA | Hands out the packs, moves between tables, asks the stall questions, plays the category manager in the afternoon |
| Groups | Nine groups, eight of four learners and one of three, the cohort's 35 in the build weeks' nine groups | Count the cards, fill one grid per pair, place the pair cards, defend one placement |

## How do the minutes run?

| Turn | Minutes | Slides | What the room does |
|---|---|---|---|
| The dots | 20 | S4 to S7 | Votes on the tea-stall sentences, counts the 2 by 2 from the dots, finds the carry bag's 0.83 |

## What does the trainer say at each turn?

**The dots.** "Ten orders at a tea stall. How many hold chai? How many hold a biscuit? How many hold
both?" Write 6, 4 and 3 in the grid. Then: "If chai and biscuit ignored each other, how many of the
4 biscuit orders would hold chai?" Wait for 60 percent of 4, 2.4. "Three do. Three over 2.4 is the
lift." Only now write the word lift.

**The carry bag.** "A carry bag goes in 9 of the 10 orders. Biscuit to bag reads 0.75. Is the bag
biscuit's best partner?" Let the room argue, then: "The bag is in 0.90 of every order. 0.75 over
0.90 is 0.83."

**The twelve carts.** Say it exactly: "A cart is a basket. Sort the pack by whether it has bread,
then count how many of those have milk. Now count how many have milk at all." Write milk 8, bread 4,
both 4 as the groups call them. Then chips and cold drink (3, 3, both 2), then ice cream and
chocolate sauce (1, 1, both 1). Name support, confidence and lift only after all three rows are on
the board.

**The sort.** Hand out the pair cards once notebook 03 has ranked the pairs above the floor and listed the thin ones below it. "Every number
on these cards is one you just computed. Place each card on the map, then pick one card your group
would defend to the growth lead." Each group reads its sentence; the room says whether the card
stays.

## What do you do when it stalls?

| The stall | The sign | What to say |
|---|---|---|
| Confidence taken as the answer | A group writes 1.00 for bread and milk and stops | "How many carts have milk whether or not they have bread?" |
| Expected count stuck | A group cannot get 2.67 | "If milk ignored bread, how many of the 4 bread carts would hold milk, with milk in 8 of 12?" |
| Lift read as a cause | "Chai sells 25 percent more biscuits" | "What would you have to change at the stall to see that?" |
| The thin pair promoted | Ice cream and chocolate sauce placed in Promote | "How many carts would the slot touch?" It shows beside ice cream only, 1,600 carts in four weeks, and the pair sits in 100 of them, against 400 at the floor |
| The trap pair promoted | Bread and milk placed in Promote because of 10,064 carts | "What would chance alone put beside bread, with milk in 71 percent of carts?" |
| A group finishes early | All nine cards placed in under ten minutes | Hand it namkeen and poha, lift 1.29 on 116 carts: watch or ignore? |

## What is on the board when the activity ends?

The ten dots' grid with 0.30, 0.50 and 1.25 beside it; the three running-count rows from the cards;
the action map with nine cards placed; and, after the afternoon, the category manager's line with the
four moves written under it.
