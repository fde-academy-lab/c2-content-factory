# Run sheet: receipts on the table, from the canteen's cards to the action-map sort

**TRAINER ONLY.** This sheet runs the day's signature activity: the room counts support, confidence
and lift by hand on the canteen's twelve invented lunch orders in chapters 1 to 4, counts them again
on twelve real Instamart carts in chapter 7, and sorts pair cards on the wall's action map in chapter
10. A trainer who did not write the day can run it from this page alone.

## Who plays which role?

| Role | Who | What they do |
|---|---|---|
| Trainer | The day's trainer | Runs the board, says the lines below, writes every count the room calls out before any formula is named |
| TA | The day's TA | Hands out the packs, moves between tables, asks the stall questions, plays the category manager in the afternoon |
| Groups | Nine groups, eight of four learners and one of three, the cohort's 35 in the build weeks' nine groups | Count the cards, fill one grid per pair, place the pair cards, defend one placement |

## What does the trainer say at each turn?

**Support.** "Twelve lunch orders from a hostel canteen. How many hold roti? How many hold sabzi? How
many hold both?" Write 4, 4 and 3 in the grid, then the roti-only, sabzi-only and neither cells, and
check the four cells add to twelve. Only now write the word support beside 3 over 12.

**Confidence.** "Put every card with dal rice in one pile. How many of those hold papad? Now put
every card with papad in a pile. How many of those hold dal rice?" Write 3 of 6 and 3 of 5 side by
side and ask why the same three orders give two numbers.

**Chance.** "If roti and sabzi ignored each other, roti's four orders would hold sabzi at the rate
all twelve do. What is a third of four?" Wait for 1.33. "Three do. Three over 1.33 is the lift."
Only now write the word lift. Then roti and chaas: "Chance gives 3, and 3 are seen."

**The popular partner.** "Every dal rice order holds chaas. Is chaas dal rice's best partner?" Let
the room argue, then: "Chaas is in 9 of the 12 orders anyway. Chance puts it in 4.5 of dal rice's
6."

**The twelve carts.** Say it exactly: "A cart is a basket. Sort the pack by whether it has bread,
then count how many of those have milk. Now count how many have milk at all." Write milk 8, bread 4,
both 4 as the groups call them. Then chips and cold drink (3, 3, both 2), then ice cream and
chocolate sauce (1, 1, both 1). The room names support, confidence and lift itself, since it counted
them on the canteen.

**The sort.** Hand out the pair cards once notebook 10 has ranked the pairs above the floor and
listed the thin ones below it. "Every number on these cards is one you just computed. Place each
card on the map, using chapter 6's four regions, then pick one card your group would defend to the
growth lead." One group reads its sentence; the room says whether the card stays.

## What do you do when it stalls?

| The stall | The sign | What to say |
|---|---|---|
| Counts added | A group writes 8 for roti and sabzi | "Find the orders that hold both: which cards are they?" |
| One direction for both | A group writes 0.50 for papad to dal rice | "How many cards are in the papad pile?" |
| Chance stuck | A group cannot get 1.33 | "If sabzi ignored roti, how many of the 4 roti orders would hold sabzi, with sabzi in 4 of 12?" |
| Lift read as a cause | "Roti makes people buy 2.25 times the sabzi" | "What would you have to change at the counter to see that?" |
| Confidence taken as the answer | A group writes 1.00 for bread and milk and stops | "How many carts have milk whether or not they have bread?" |
| The thin pair promoted | Ice cream and chocolate sauce placed in Promote | "How many carts would the slot touch?" It shows beside ice cream only, 1,600 carts in four weeks, and the pair sits in 100 of them, against 400 at the floor |
| The trap pair promoted | Bread and milk placed in Promote because of 10,064 carts | "What would chance alone put beside bread, with milk in 71 percent of carts?" |
| A group finishes early | All nine cards placed in under two minutes | Hand it namkeen and poha, lift 1.29 on 116 carts: watch or ignore? |

## What is on the board when the activity ends?

The concept map with its five boxes numbered; the canteen's 2 by 2 with 0.25, 0.75 and 2.25 beside
it; chaas's row of confidences and lifts; the three running-count rows from the cart cards; the
action map with nine cards placed; and, after the afternoon, the category manager's line with the
four moves written under it.
