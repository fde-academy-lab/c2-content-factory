# Role card: the category manager who wants milk in every slot

**TRAINER ONLY.** The TA plays this role in the afternoon's unguided block (half two, S40), and the
trainer plays it in the fallback's second transcript. Read it once before the session; say the lines
in your own voice.

Swiggy Instamart, Pune. Illustrative case: the company is real, the situation and the data are
synthetic, built for this class.

## Who is the category manager, and what do they want?

The category manager owns dairy and staples for Pune. Toned milk is their top seller: it sits in 71
percent of carts and brings shoppers back every day. Since 7 September the strip has shown milk
beside nearly everything, and they want it kept there, in every slot.

**Partly right, on purpose.** Milk does anchor the cart. What they miss is that the slot reminds
nobody: milk slots click at 1.2 percent against 6.4 percent for everything else, and milk's lift with
every common item sits near one.

## What do you say, and when?

| Moment | Line to say |
|---|---|
| Opening, to the whole room | "Milk is our top seller. It is in 71 percent of carts. Put it in every slot." |
| To a group that quotes confidence 0.74 | "So three in four bread buyers take milk. That sounds like a bundle to me." |
| To a group that quotes 1.2 percent | "Shoppers see milk and buy it anyway. The strip keeps it in front of them." |
| To a group that offers only a number | "Numbers are fine. What do I get if I give up the slots?" |
| To a group that concedes everything | "So milk was never right? Then why is it our top seller?" |

## What answer should move you, and what should not?

Concede only to an answer that makes Tuesday's four moves in order:

1. **Restate** until you agree: "You want milk on the strip because it anchors the cart."
2. **Answer from the evidence:** milk slots click at 1.2 percent against 6.4, and milk's lift with
   bread is 1.04, so the slot shows shoppers what they were buying anyway.
3. **Concede what is true:** milk belongs in the cart, which is why it needs no reminder; keep it on
   the home screen and in search.
4. **Say what would change their mind:** a two-zone test where milk's slots clicked like the rest
   would put milk back.

Do not move for an answer that calls milk a bad product, that drops milk from search or the home
screen, or that quotes Rs 80,197 of sales as the strip's gain.

## How do you close the exchange?

Say "Fine. Show me the two-zone test on Friday." to the group whose answer made all four moves, and
"I am not convinced yet." to every other group, naming the move it skipped.
