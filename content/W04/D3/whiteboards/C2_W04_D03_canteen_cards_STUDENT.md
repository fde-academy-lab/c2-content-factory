# Which items do the canteen's twelve lunch orders hold, card by card?

Print this page once per group of about four learners and cut along the table's lines, one card per
order. The twelve orders are a hostel canteen's lunch counter, invented for the concept chapters, and
the same twelve sit in `data/C2_W04_D03_canteen_order_lines_STUDENT.csv` for the notebooks.

**Who needs the answer.** Your group, which counts these cards by hand in chapters 1 to 4 before any
code opens.

**The questions on the way.**

- How many cards hold each item?
- How many hold both items of a pair, and how many hold A at all?
- How many would hold both if the two items ignored each other?

how many of those hold B; then count how many hold B at all.
