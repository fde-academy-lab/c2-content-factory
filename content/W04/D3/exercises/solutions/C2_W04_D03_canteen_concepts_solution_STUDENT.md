## What does the set test?

It tests whether each concept holds on its own example before the case leans on it: the denominator
of a support, the direction of a confidence, chance and lift, the popular partner, pairs from order
lines, the floor, and the cost line of a promotion. Four of the seven items are design calls, a
decision with a reason, rather than a sum.

## Why is each key right, and each other letter wrong?

## Which item is worth arguing about?

Q6. In this month nothing pulls any item toward another, yet gulab jamun and jalebi read lift 8.57 on
2 orders. The watch list is still the right call, because nobody running a shop knows which month
they are in: a trial with a pass rule set first would show no pull and close the entry. Promoting on
the number would have printed a card for luck.
