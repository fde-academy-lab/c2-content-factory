# What would you pay a team for in a product you use every day, and what stops it being gamed?

*Illustrative case: the company is real, the situation and the data are synthetic, built for this class.*

**The ticket, in short.** Uber, Hyderabad, the driver app. From Monday 31 August 2026 an enrolled
driver who stays online through a whole peak window is guaranteed Rs 300 per peak online hour, and
Uber tops up the gap. Six weeks later peak online hours were up 24 percent, completed peak trips up 3
percent, and trips per peak online hour down from 1.90 to 1.58, with Rs 1.9 crore paid in top-up. The
room redesigned the scheme today: pay for completed peak trips per enrolled driver online at peak,
guarded by the share of offers accepted, the median rider wait and the top-up per completed trip.

**Who needs the answer.** The city head, who hears the redesign on one slide at Thursday's review
with three people who each lose something under it; Tuesday rehearses that slide, and it starts
from the one number you bring. Your own definition is the practice: a definition you can write for a
product you know is one you can defend for a product you are handed.

**The questions on the way.** What does the redesign reward, said to a driver in one sentence? What
number would you pay a team for in a product you use every day, written on seven lines? How could
that team hit it while the product got worse? Which single number carries the redesign on
Tuesday's slide? What is the proxy test, in one line?

About 45 minutes, alone, on one page. Bring the page to Tuesday's session.

---

## What does the redesign reward, said to a driver in one sentence?

Write one sentence a driver waiting at a signal would understand, with no formula and no metric
name: what earns the top-up under the new rule, and what no longer earns it. Then read it aloud
once. If a word in it needs explaining, replace the word.

---

## What number would you pay a team for in a product you use every day?

Choose one product you open every day: a food-delivery app, a music app, a payments app, a
ride-hailing app as a rider, a quick-commerce app, or your college's library portal. Pick the number
its team would most like to grow, and write it on the seven lines.

| Line | In plain words | Your product |
|---|---|---|
| Numerator | What is counted on top | |
| Denominator | What the count is divided by, with that count printed beside the rate | |
| Window | The stretch of time each count covers | |
| Exclusions | What is left out of both counts, and why | |
| What it does not reward | The behaviour the number refuses to pay for, named out loud | |
| Guardrails, two | Numbers that must not get worse while your number rises | |
| Gaming note | For each guardrail, the way of hitting your number it catches | |

You will not have the product's data, so write no numbers you do not know. Every line is a choice,
and each choice is yours to defend.

---

## How could that team hit the number while the product got worse?

Under your table, write the two ways a team paid on your number could hit it while customers were
served worse. Next to each, name the line of your definition that catches it. If neither line
catches one of them, add a guardrail and say what it would catch.

---

## Which single number carries the redesign on Tuesday's slide?

Tuesday puts the redesign on one slide in front of the driver-operations lead, the marketing lead
and the finance partner, and every slide is carried by one number. Choose the one you would put at
the top from the day's case, write it with its denominator, its window and its before and after
values, and in one sentence say why that number and none of the others. Notebooks 01 to 03 print
every number you might choose.

---

## What is the proxy test, in one line?

Write the proxy test in one line of your own words, then apply it once to the number you chose for
your product.

---

One page with five parts: the driver's sentence, your product's seven lines, the two ways of gaming
it with the lines that catch them, the one number for Tuesday's slide with its sentence, and the
proxy test in one line. Check it against `C2_W04_D01_selfcheck_STUDENT.md` before you bring it.
