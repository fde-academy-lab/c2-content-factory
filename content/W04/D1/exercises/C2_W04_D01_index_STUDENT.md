# Which exercise answers which of Monday's questions, and when do you sit it?

*The six concept sets use examples invented for the morning's chapters, labelled where they appear. The Uber case is illustrative: the company is real, the situation and the data are synthetic, built for this class.*

**The ticket, in short.** Uber, Hyderabad, the driver app. From Monday 31 August 2026 an enrolled
driver who stays online through a whole peak window is guaranteed Rs 300 per peak online hour, and
Uber tops up the gap. Six weeks later peak online hours are up 24 percent, completed peak trips up 3
percent, and trips per peak online hour down from 1.90 to 1.58, with Rs 1.9 crore paid in top-up. The
city head asks, by Friday: is the scheme working, which metric should it pay for, and is anyone
gaming it?

**Who needs the answer.** The city head, who signs the scheme's next six weeks on Friday, and you,
since every exercise below practises one of the day's six topics or one of the questions the day
climbs to answer hers, and working through them shows whether you can answer it yourself.

**The questions on the way.** Which file answers which question, and when? Which item tests which of
the day's six topics? Which item sits at which level of the day's ladder? How many items ask for a
design call? When do the solutions open? What shape does every answer line take?

## When do the solutions open?

Each concept set's Q1 is called aloud at the end of its chapter, and the trainer says the key and
one reason; the other two items and every concept set's solution open at the end of the practice
lab. The guided definition's answers are called aloud in chapter 9, and its solution opens at the end
of that chapter. Round 3's solution opens at the close of the trainer's afternoon. The Kalpa set, the
lead-and-lag drill and the lab set open at the end of the practice lab, after the TA has walked the
lab notebook's two your-turn cells. The take-home is walked at the start of Tuesday.

line into the class chat. A three-item set posts three letters, the guided definition and round 3
four, the Kalpa set and the lab set five; each file prints its own shape above its first item.
