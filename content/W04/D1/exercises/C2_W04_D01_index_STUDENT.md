# Which exercise answers which of Monday's questions, and when do you sit it?

*Illustrative case: the company is real, the situation and the data are synthetic, built for this class.*

**The ticket, in short.** Uber, Hyderabad, the driver app. From Monday 31 August 2026 an enrolled
driver who stays online through a whole peak window is guaranteed Rs 300 per peak online hour, and
Uber tops up the gap. Six weeks later peak online hours are up 24 percent, completed peak trips up 3
percent, and trips per peak online hour down from 1.90 to 1.58, with Rs 1.9 crore paid in top-up. The
city head asks, by Friday: is the scheme working, which metric should it pay for, and is anyone
gaming it?

**Who needs the answer.** The city head, who signs the scheme's next six weeks on Friday, and you,
since every exercise below practises one of the questions the day climbs to answer hers, and working
through them shows whether you can answer it yourself.

**The questions on the way.** Which file answers which question, and when? Which item sits at which
level of the day's ladder? How many items ask for a design call? When do the solutions open? What
shape does every answer line take?

## Which item sits at which level of the day's ladder?

The day climbs six levels: L1 counts by hand, L2 computes on the full data, L3 finds the trap, L4
names the caveats, L5 decides and prices, L6 answers at interview depth. The twelve-row count (L1)
and the full-data count (L2) are done in the decks and notebooks 01 and 02, so the exercises start
where those leave off, with one code item for anyone who wants L2 again. Each item carries the
interview question it descends from, tagged the way the week's interview list tags it: **[S]** a
staple asked everywhere, **[F]** frequent in GCC and product screens, **[D]** a differentiator.

| Level | Item | What it asks | Interview question it descends from |
|---|---|---|---|
| L2 | Lab set, Q1 | Which lines, in which order, print the city's rate? | [S] How would you measure the success of a new feature? |

| L4 | Guided, Q3 | Which exclusions belong in the definition? | [F] What makes a metric gameable, and how do you write one that is not? |
