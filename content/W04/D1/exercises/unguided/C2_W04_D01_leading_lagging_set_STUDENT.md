# Which numbers warn while a team can still act, and which only confirm what already happened?

*Kalpa Retail is the programme's fictional client, and its numbers here are names with no values. Balaji Kirana and the chase are invented for chapter 3 and chapter 5, with every number in them.*

**The example, in short.** In chapter 3's chase (invented) the side needed 160 to win; after over 10
it stood at 60 for 3, so the runs an over needed had reached 10.0, ten overs before the scoreboard
confirmed a loss by 12. A **leading** number moves before the north star while the team can still
act on it; a **lagging** number confirms the north star after the result is fixed. Kalpa's growth
plan chases one north star: delivered orders per active customer per month.

**Who needs the answer.** Kalpa's growth team, who read a dashboard every Monday and act on it that
week, and the owner of Balaji Kirana (invented), who can read one number every morning. A dashboard
of numbers that only confirm lets a drift run for weeks before anyone sees it.

**The questions on the way.** Which of eight Kalpa numbers move before the north star? Which reason
defends two of those calls? Which one number should the shop's owner read each morning?

Q1 is called aloud at the end of chapter 3, with the trainer, and two learners defend one call each;
Q2 and Q3 are sat in the practice lab. Every item has one right answer. Items marked **Design** ask
for the best-fit approach, the fact that would switch it, or the second route that would confirm a
number.

## Which reason defends two of those calls?

A call on a dashboard is only as good as the reason said beside it, because the next person to read
the dashboard moves a number only if the reason convinces them.

### Q2. Which reason defends calling number 1 leading and number 2 lagging?
