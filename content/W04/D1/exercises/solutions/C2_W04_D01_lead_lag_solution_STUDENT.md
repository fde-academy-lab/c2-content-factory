# Which letters sort the city's numbers into leading and lagging, and pair each north star with its guardrail?

Answers: 1a 2c 3d

*Illustrative case: the company is real, the situation and the data are synthetic, built for this class.*

Uber's Hyderabad city team pays drivers a peak-hour floor of Rs 300 per online hour since 31 August
2026. Six weeks on, hours are up 24 percent, trips up 3 percent, and trips per peak online hour down
from 1.90 to 1.58. The city team reads one dashboard every Monday, and this set sorts eight of its
numbers by when they move and pairs three proposed north stars with the guardrail each needs.

## What does the set test about leading numbers and guardrails?

A leading number moves before the north star and can still be acted on this week; a lagging number
adds up what already happened. A guardrail is chosen by the gaming it catches: for each proposed north
star, the question is how a driver could hit it while riders were served no better, and which number would
move when that happens. The case shows the lead in time: acceptance settled at its new level, 0.729,
in the launch week, while gross bookings per online hour took three weeks to fall from Rs 271 to
Rs 259.1.

## Why is each key right, and why does each other option fail?

| Item | Key | The question in one line | Why the key holds | Why each other option fails |

## Which item is worth arguing about?

Item 3, the spare guardrail. G4, total peak online hours, is the guardrail a hurried group writes
for the hours north star, because it sounds like supply. It is another growth count: it rises with
the idling it is supposed to catch. The question to ask of any guardrail is what it would catch, and
for G4 the answer is nothing.

## Where does a north star without the right guardrail show up at work?

Swiggy Instamart's Maxxsaver unlocks extra discounts once a cart passes a threshold. In the quarter to
30 June 2025 over 28 percent of Swiggy's monthly transacting users used it, Instamart's average order
value rose 16 percent quarter on quarter to Rs 612, and Swiggy wrote that cart consolidation led by
Maxxsaver lowered order growth (Swiggy's Q1 FY26 shareholder letter, checked 12 October 2026). A
north star of orders per month, read with no guardrail on value, would have shown that change as a
fall.
