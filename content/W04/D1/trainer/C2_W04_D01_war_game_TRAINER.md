# How does a trainer who did not write the day run Monday's war game?

**TRAINER ONLY.** The war game is the day's signature activity: three city-team roles, each partly
right, and a definition that has to survive them. Round 1 opens the day, round 2 is the morning's
three chapters, and round 3 closes the trainer's time.

**Who needs the answer.** A trainer running the day cold, with the role cards
(`trainer/C2_W04_D01_role_cards_TRAINER.md`) in hand.

**The questions on the way.** Who plays what? What materials does each group need? What happens in
round 1, minute by minute? What does round 2 add? What happens in round 3? What do you do when it
stalls?

---

## Who plays what?

| Role | Who plays it | What they do |
|---|---|---|
| The driver-operations lead | The trainer, or a learner reading the card | Proposes peak online hours; in round 3, attacks with "drivers will log off" |
| The marketing lead | The trainer, or a learner reading the card | Proposes enrolled drivers; in round 3, attacks with "changing terms looks like bait" |
| The finance partner | The trainer, or a learner reading the card | Proposes top-up per completed trip; in round 3, attacks with "it is the monsoon's end" |
| The groups | The room, in nine groups of about four (35 learners) | Attack the roles' numbers in round 1; write, price and defend the redesign in round 3 |
| The scorer | One learner per group | Holds the proxy-test card and scores each attack in round 3 |

If learners read the roles, pick three who will commit to the role's voice, give them the card five
minutes before the day starts, and tell them their job is to be partly right.

## What happens in round 1, minute by minute?

Fifteen minutes, deck S1 to S5, before any method is taught.

| Minutes | What happens | What you say |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Read the ticket (S1) in the order it is written | "This is the whole ticket. Company, what launched, what moved, the data, the ask, the caveats." |
| 2 | The week map (S2) and where today ends (S3) | "By the close, one sentence with three blanks will be filled from the data." |
| 2 | The board's four questions (S4) | "Every number anyone proposes today gets these four questions." |
| 8 | The three roles state their numbers (S5); each group gives one way to game each | "Driver operations wants hours. Marketing wants enrolled drivers. Finance wants top-up per trip. Each group: one way each number could rise while riders were served no better." |

Take one answer per role from three different groups. Write each on the board beside the role's
number. Do not correct anything yet: round 2 is where the room learns to attack with a method.

---

## What does round 2 add?

Round 2 is the morning's three chapters. Name it once at the start of chapter 1: "Round 2 is the
morning. By lunch you will attack with numbers." Each chapter close adds a region to
the concept board, and each region answers one of round 1's attacks with the data.

---

## What happens in round 3?

Thirty minutes, deck S38 to S43, after lunch.

| Minutes | What happens | What you say |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | The brief (S38): each group writes the redesign on the template, prices it, and answers the roles | "Your definition survives when every role's attack is answered by one of its lines or a guardrail." |
| 14 | Groups write the seven lines, answer the four items of the redesign set, and write the saving with its assumption | Visit each group as one role, rotating; read the role's attack in the role's voice |
| 3 | The three attacks (S39), read aloud in the roles' voices | "Each attack is partly right. Name the true part before you answer the rest." |
| 5 | S40 and S41: which line answers "drivers will log off"; where the acceptance floor sits | "The median wait is the line that moves for riders; the floor sits between 0.46 and 0.78." |
| 3 | S42: the saving as a range | "A group that wrote Rs 1.16 crore assumed nobody changes. What does an idle driver do on Monday under the new rule?" |
| 2 | Scoring: each scorer reads its group's tally of answered, conceded and lost | "Lost means nothing on your sheet meets it. Tuesday's slide has to win that role back." |

S43 (Maxxsaver and Eternal) runs if the 30 minutes allow; cut it first.

---

## What do you do when it stalls?

| Stall | The one move |
|---|---|
| Round 1: nobody can say how hours could be gamed | Show the canteen token (S8 early): "who controls the count?" |
| Round 1: the room defends hours | Point at drivers E and F on the twelve rows: hours 10 then 16 and 10 then 14, trips 18 then 3 and 17 then 2 |
| Round 3: a group writes a guardrail that is another growth count, such as bookings per driver | Ask: "what would that guardrail catch?" |
| Round 3: a group drops the floor to answer finance | Read the marketing lead's card: the floor was the promise |
| Round 3: a group writes "saves Rs 1.16 crore" | Ask what the 180 idle drivers do under the new rule; point at S42 |
| Round 3: a group cannot answer driver operations | Ask what happened to the other 880 drivers' trips per hour (1.90 in both windows) |
