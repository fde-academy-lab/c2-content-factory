## What does the lab ask, and what is the order of work?

The head of Retail-Plus asks (the row): "Frequency fell in my tier. Is it everyone, or the members who
joined during the monsoon sale and never came back? And the app team says the reorder feature has
been fixed, but our blended retention number has not moved. Where are we actually losing people?"
Kalpa counts a member as active in a month when she opened the app at least once, since v4 cannot
carry the orders-based version (the facts file's correction); say so if a learner asks why the
definition differs from Zomato's, and that the head's "frequency" is read through it. The table has
no channel column: the members whose membership started in August are the monsoon-sale members (the
case file's August monsoon cohort). The auto-reorder feature placed repeat orders for members on its
own from 10 to 30 September (Monday's window); the app team says it has since been fixed, so the
window is the stretch to check.

## Which plants should the your-turn cells find, and what do you do if nobody finds one?

The plants stay on this page until a learner's own cell prints them. After that, the room may say
them (decision `w4-finding-once-found`), and the Kalpa set's after-lab page states them.

| Plant | What the full data says | If nobody finds it |
|---|---|---|
| Blended retention flat near 41 | 30 of 74 in July, 36 of 88 in August, 49 of 120 in September: 40.5, 40.9 and 40.8 percent | Ask "who joined before each month, and how many of them opened the app?" |
| Every cohort falls | Each cohort's active share falls month by month; May's runs 7, 6 and 5 of 13 | Ask "read one cohort's row across: does it ever rise?" |
| The monsoon cohort weakest in month one | August, the largest cohort at 32 members: 26 of 32 in September, 81 percent, against 13 of 14 for July's and 12 of 13 for June's | Ask "line the cohorts up at month one, not in one calendar month" |
| Add-to-cart to checkout falls in the window | 28 of 34 before 20 August (82 percent), 10 of 12 from 20 August to 9 September (83), 7 of 11 from 10 to 30 September (64) | Ask "split the carts by the dates Monday's lab fixed" |
| A step members can skip | 145 of 469 sessions, 31 percent, have no search | Ask "how many sessions reach a cart without a search?" |
| Two units, two numbers | September's checkout: 12 of 153 sessions (7.8 percent) and 12 of 49 active members (24.5 percent) | Ask "per what?", then "per whom?" |

**The doubt to say aloud.** The window's fall rests on 11 carts: at the earlier 10 in 12, about 9
would have checked out and 7 did, so about 2 checkouts are missing in three weeks. A strong group
calls the step to check first and asks for the next three weeks to confirm it; a group that
rebuilds the checkout on 11 carts has overread it.

The three lines a group brings back: whether it is a cohort problem or an everyone problem, with the
August cohort and its size; the step with its dates and its counts; the unit of each rate.

## Where do learners stall, and which hint unlocks each?

The TA's first hint for any cohort question: "when did she enter?". Learners stall on month numbering,
counting the joining month as month one.

The practice set's keys are 1c, 2a, 3b, 4a, 5d and 6b.
