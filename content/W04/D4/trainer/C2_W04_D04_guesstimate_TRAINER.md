# Guesstimate of the day: how many people ride the Delhi Metro on a weekday?

**TRAINER ONLY.** Five minutes, in the interview room slot or as the lab's warm-up. The room
estimates first; the anchor is revealed last. The catch is the day's lesson in another city: a number
is only as good as its unit, as journeys, boardings and people are three different counts of one
crowd.

## How does a strong candidate run it?

| Step | What they say | The numbers |
|---|---|---|
| Clarify | "People, journeys or boardings? I'll count people who ride at least once on an ordinary weekday, then convert to journeys to check." | |
| Map | "People in the area the metro serves, times the share who ride on a weekday." | |
| Assume, with ranges | "About 2 crore people within reach, from 1.5 to 2.5 crore; 15 percent ride on a weekday, from 10 to 20." | Every input is an assumption, said as one |
| Calculate | "2 crore x 0.15 is about 30 lakh riders. Most ride there and back, so about 60 lakh journeys." | About 15 lakh to 50 lakh riders across the ranges |
| Validate | "DMRC reported 64.06 lakh average daily passenger journeys in 2026, so my 60 lakh journeys is about 6 percent low." | The Tribune, 9 August 2026, citing DMRC (checked 12 October 2026) |
| The catch | "The record 82,44,903 on 17 September 2026 counts boardings. If a boarding counts each line used, as DMRC's line count has since 2018, a rider who changes lines once counts twice, so it can't validate a journey estimate." | Republic World, 18 September 2026, citing DMRC (checked 12 October 2026); the 2018 change, PTI via LatestLY (checked 12 October 2026) |

## What does each wrong turn look like, and what do you say?

| The wrong turn | What to say |
|---|---|
| Starts from trains and stations with no load factor | "How full is a train at noon against at 9? Which number would you check first?" |
| Validates people against journeys | "How many journeys does a commuter make in a day?" |
| Validates against the boardings record | "What does DMRC count as a boarding when a rider changes lines?" |
| Gives one number with no range | "Which input are you least sure of, and what happens to the answer at its ends?" |
| Treats the record day as an ordinary weekday | "What happened on 17 September? A bus strike sent riders to the metro." |
