# Which exercise answers which question on Thursday, and when is each sat?

Thursday teaches five topics, in the curriculum row's words: cohort construction; funnel stages,
drop-off, denominators; retention curves: shape and floor; blended numbers against cohort truth; and
the fix-first decision and its evidence. Each is taught first on an invented gym or college, then
applied to the day's case, Zomato Gold in Chennai, where the membership dashboard reads month-two
retention flat at 38 percent while every signup cohort falls with age, and members' add-to-cart to
order on Android fell from 58 to 44 percent after a release on 14 September. Illustrative case: the
company is real, the situation and the data are synthetic, built for this class. The exercises climb
the day's ladder from a hand count (L1) to an interview call (L6), and the Kalpa set moves the method
to the programme's client.

**Who needs the answer.** You, to know which set to open, when, what each one practises, and which
items to redo on a topic that did not land.

Each concept drill takes three to five minutes, with letters posted. When a chapter runs long, its
drill moves to the lab, and the topic's row in the first table says which later items practise the
same idea.

## In what order do the day's two written pages come?

Two pages are written, each with the same five lines: the call, the evidence, the cost, the
objection answered and what would change it. SECTION 8 builds the first such page on the college.
The fix-first set's page is for Zomato Gold's membership lead, in the afternoon. In the lab, notebook
12 ends on three sentences for the TA, which become the first lines of the second page, the Kalpa
set's page for the head of Retail-Plus, written after its items 2 to 4. The practice set and the
Kaapi and Co. drill come last and ask for letters only.

## How many items ask for a design call?

Twenty-two of the forty-two items are design calls: a best-fit comparison, a sizing, the fact that would
change the call, or a second route. Each is marked "a design call" beside its level.

## Where are the answers?

Every set has a solutions file of the same name in `solutions/`, opened after the set is sat. Each
gives the answer letters, why each key holds and why each other letter fails, and the item worth
arguing about. The afternoon and Kalpa solutions also show what a strong page says.
