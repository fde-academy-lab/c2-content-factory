# Which of the college's two problems goes first, and what does its cost line say?

The same college as SECTIONs 4 and 8. Its dean has one admissions team for one fix before the next
cycle, and two problems: 200 of 1,000 applications never sat the entrance test this cycle, the same
20 percent as every cycle, and offer acceptance fell from 90 to 75 percent. SECTION 8 split the fall by the student's home
state, and notebook 08 sized it. The college and every number on this page are invented for
practice; the fee is an assumption.

This drill runs in the lab, on paper, with no code, in about three minutes; it reads what SECTION 8
and notebook 08 built.

**How to read an item.** Its level runs from L1, a count by hand, to L6, the call an interviewer asks for; "a design call" asks you to choose a way and say why. "Screens" are interviews, and a GCC is a global capability centre, a multinational company's own technology and analytics centre in India.

**Who needs the answer.** The dean, who signs off one fix. The wrong fix leaves the lost students
lost for another year, and a cost line with no range or the wrong unit is the first thing the
finance office strikes out.

**The questions on the way.**

- Which problem goes first, and which fact would change the call?
- Which cost line belongs on the page?

| Offers accepted, percent of those shortlisted | Two cycles ago | Last cycle | This cycle |
|---|---|---|---|
| In-state students | 92.1 | 92.0 | 90.0 |
| Students from other states | 86.4 | 86.7 | 50.0 |
| Days allowed to pay the fee after the offer | 21 | 21 | 7 |

At last cycle's rates this cycle's shortlist would have accepted 360; 300 did, so 60 fewer, 55 of
them from other states. Eight in ten of those who accept join, so the fall is 48 fewer students; the
joiners counted directly read 288 last cycle and 240 this cycle. A first-year fee of Rs 1 to 1.5
lakh is an assumption.

**What you post.** Two letters in item order, with no spaces, in this shape:

## Which problem goes first, and what would change the call?

This comes up at work whenever two problems compete for one team: the problem whose step moved goes
first, and the call names the one fact that would change it.

### Q1. Which order of work holds, and which fact would change the call?

*Level L5, a design call · asked in product-company screens.*

a) The test's no-show first, as 200 beats 60; changed if acceptance fell again next cycle
b) Other-state acceptance first; changed if it had been falling before the deadline was cut
c) Both at once, half the team on each; changed only if the team could not be split in two
d) Neither until a second cycle confirms the fall; changed if the fee rose and joiners fell

## What does the cost line carry?

This comes up at work when a call goes to the people who pay for the fix: the cost is counted in the
unit the business is paid in, with its assumption named and ranged.

### Q2. Which cost line belongs on the dean's page?

*Level L5, a design call · asked in GCC analytics screens.*

a) 48 fewer students: Rs 48 to 72 lakh of first-year fees, at an assumed Rs 1 to 1.5 lakh
b) 60 fewer acceptances at Rs 1.2 lakh each: Rs 72 lakh, with every acceptance priced
c) 200 applications that never sat the test, at Rs 1.2 lakh each, the bigger count by far
d) 48 fewer students at exactly Rs 1.2 lakh each: Rs 57.6 lakh, with no range given at all
