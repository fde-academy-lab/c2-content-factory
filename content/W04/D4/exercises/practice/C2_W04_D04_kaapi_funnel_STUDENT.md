## Which step only looks like a loss or a win?

This comes up at work whenever a funnel is read by a team that owns one of its steps: before any step
is blamed or praised, each rate has to divide two counts of the same people or sessions.

### Q1. Which step reads above 100 percent per session, and why?

*Level L3 · asked in every analytics screen.*

a) Search to product view, 120 percent, since the banner opens products without a search
b) Product view to cart, 33 percent, since browsing drags the rate below the rest
c) Cart to order, 60 percent, since some carts are paid for twice in one session
d) Search to product view, 120 percent, since a shopper views several products per search

### Q2. The search team asks how often a search leads to a product: which rate answers them?

*Level L3, a design call · asked in product-company screens.*

a) Product-view sessions over search sessions, 120 percent
b) Search sessions over product-view sessions, 83 percent
c) Product views among the sessions that searched
d) Product-view sessions over every session that opened the app

### Q3. Counted in customers, what does product view to cart read, against 33 percent per session?

*Level L3 · asked in every analytics screen.*

a) 33 percent, the same in both units
b) 58 percent, 1,400 of the 2,400 customers who viewed a product
c) 67 percent, 1,400 of the 2,100 customers who searched
d) 23 percent, 1,400 customers over the 6,000 product-view sessions

## Is the fall in the ordering, or in the counting?

This comes up at work the week after every app release: a step falls, and the first question is
whether customers changed or the log did.

### Q4. Cart to order fell from 60 to 48 percent in the week after an app update: which finding would say the update broke the counting while ordering held?

*Level L4, a design call · asked in GCC analytics screens.*

a) The fall holds when the cut between before and after moves a week in either direction
b) Only customers on one platform fell, and customers on the other platforms held
c) The fall sits in completing a payment, while starting the payment held steady
d) Paid orders in the shop's billing system held while the app's order events fell
