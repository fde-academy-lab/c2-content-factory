## Does your paragraph carry the four things?

A strong paragraph names numbers from your weekly table, says what changed with the grain and why,
says that the Android fall still starts in the release week, and ends on one sentence the membership
lead can repeat. A weak one quotes the monthly 38, calls the sale week's 53 percent a strong cohort,
or says the weekly table "contradicts" the monthly one without saying which sentence moved and why.
