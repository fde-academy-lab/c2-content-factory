# Exercises, Week 1 Wednesday: which Q1 figure is right?

Every exercise today puts Anand's question back to you: which figure is right, how you know, and
whether his analyst can follow every decision. There is one set per chapter, each opened by the
notebook of the same number, and the sets climb with the day. Items marked Design ask for the
best-fit approach, a sizing or the fact that would change your call.
