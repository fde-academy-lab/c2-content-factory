# Exercises, Week 1 Wednesday: which Q1 figure is right?

Every exercise today puts Anand's question back to you: which figure is right, how you know, and
whether his analyst can follow every decision. The ERP in these files is the enterprise resource
planning system Finance books orders in, and its export is the file the day cleans. There is one set
per chapter, each opened by the notebook of the same number, and the sets climb with the day. Items
marked Design ask you to combine two of the day's ideas or to size the options yourself before you
choose: 20 of today's 40 items.
