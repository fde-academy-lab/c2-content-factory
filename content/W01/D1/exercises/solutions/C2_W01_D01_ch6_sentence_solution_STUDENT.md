## What does the set test about the sentence Meera signs?

"Bought once in this window" is a fact, and "lost" is a claim about orders the window cannot see.
The 7 customers who came back took a median of 45 days to do it, and 9 of the 16 one-time buyers
ordered fewer than 45 days before 26 September, so they have not had a typical customer's time to
return. The file supports three groups: 7 came back, 7 are past the usual gap without a second
order, and 9 are too recent to judge, which makes "70 percent lost" at most 7 of 23. The sentence
carries its evidence, branch, caveat and ask, and the design items ask which form fits the reader,
which second route checks the count, and what data would narrow the caveat.

## Which item is worth arguing about?

Item 2. Some will say 70 percent is true. Seventy percent did buy once in this window, and the file
cannot show whether they are lost; marketing will ask how long Kalpa's customers usually take to come
back. The window also cuts the gap short, since a customer who took 100 days to return could
not show up inside 88, so 45 days is a floor and more of the 16 may still be on their way back.

## Where does this pattern show up at work?

Klarna reported that its assistant handled two-thirds of customer-service chats in its first month
