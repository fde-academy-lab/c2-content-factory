# Solution: chapter 6, the sentence Meera acts on

Answers: 1c 2a 3d 4c 5b

## The idea being tested

"Bought once in this window" is a fact and "lost" is a claim about the future the window cannot see.
The seven customers who came back took a median of 45 days, and 9 of the 16 one-time buyers bought
inside the last 45 days, so the file supports 7 came back, 7 had time and did not, and 9 are too
recent to judge. The sentence carries evidence, branch, caveat and ask in that order.

## The part worth arguing about

Item 1. Some will say 70 percent is true. It is true that 70 percent bought once; it is not true that
they are lost, and marketing will ask how long customers take to come back.
