# Which letters answer chapter 5's set on the branch Meera opens first?

Answers: 1c 2a 3d 4b 5c

## What does the set test?

Meera's growth plan concerns the three consumer segments, Retail-Core, Retail-Plus and Student, so it
is sized on the consumer view, the orders whose segment is one of those three: Rs 64,810 booked this
quarter, so 15 percent is about Rs 74,532, which is Rs 9,722 more. On that base the plan asks
little of either customer branch: 15 percent more customers who buy like today's, or 15 percent more
orders from the same customers, which is about three in ten of the consumer one-time buyers returning
once. Frequency is the branch to open first, because those customers exist, 7 of the 23 already came
back, and a reminder to people on file is the cheapest test. Whichever branch is funded, lifts
multiply: two 10 percent lifts make 21 percent, Rs 78,420, where adding them says Rs 77,772.

## Which item is worth arguing about?

Item 1. Some will argue that the plan should be sized on all booked revenue, since that is the total
the board reads. The board can read it, and the plan's offers still reach only the consumer
segments, so Rs 81,722 would have to come from customers who book Rs 64,810 today, 126 percent
growth for a 15 percent plan.

**Kavya's review.** "Recompute through the tree anything someone adds up, and say what base the plan
sits on before anyone argues about its size."

## Where does this pattern show up at work?

Flipkart launched Flipkart Black at Rs 1,499 a year in 2025, and Amazon offers Prime in India from
Rs 399 to Rs 1,499 a year: both pay existing customers to come back more often, a bet on the
frequency branch by companies that could have spent the same money on acquisition.
