# Solution: chapter 5, which branch first

Answers: 1b 2d 3b 4a 5c

## The idea being tested

Sized on the 29 everyday consumer orders, since no retention offer repeats the order at the top of
chapter 4's sort, the plan asks little of either customer branch: 3.3 more customers, or 4.35 more
orders from the 22 already on file. Frequency is the branch to open first because those customers exist and 7 of them
already came back, so it is the cheapest to test. Whichever branch is funded, lifts multiply: two 10
percent lifts make 21 percent, and a 15 percent discount with 10 percent more orders loses 6.5
percent of revenue.

## Item by item

| Item | Key | Why it holds | Why the others fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | b | The plan is a growth plan, and a retention offer brings back everyday orders; on the 29 everyday orders, 29 x 1.15 = 33.35, so about 4.35 more at the everyday mean of Rs 2,235. | a counts orders right and values each at the booked mean of Rs 18,160, which chapter 4 showed no typical order is. c keeps the top order inside the 21 delivered orders, so each extra order would be valued at the Rs 24,800 delivered mean. d plans on the exception. |
| 2 | d | 1.10 x 1.10 = 1.21: Rs 78,420 against the slide's Rs 77,772. | a is the slide's addition. b and c have no arithmetic behind them. |
| 3 | b | Revenue is price times quantity: 0.85 x 1.10 = 0.935. | a counts the orders and forgets the price. c adds percentages. d assumes the effects are equal and opposite. |
| 4 | a | Customers falling while frequency held would say acquisition is the branch that is short. | b would strengthen the frequency call. c and d are leaks and order value, not the choice between the two customer branches. |
| 5 | c | A reminder to people already on the list, measured against a group that did not get it, costs little and shows what it added. | a asks why without measuring what a nudge adds, and people who have not returned rarely answer. b costs 6.5 percent of revenue even with a 10 percent lift. d moves price, a different branch. |

## The part worth arguing about

Item 4. A room may say "if frequency fell, fund frequency harder". That is reasonable, and it does
not move the first branch; the item asks what would.
