## One thing to think about before you arrive

Revenue fell. Only two things can have happened at the top of the tree, and they call for completely
different responses:

1. **Fewer customers bought at all.** That is an acquisition problem, and marketing's Rs 12 crore
   is aimed at it.
2. **The same customers bought less often.** That is a retention problem, and no amount of
   acquisition money fixes it.

Write one sentence tonight on how you would tell those two apart with the orders file you already
have. Bring the sentence. You will be asked for it.
