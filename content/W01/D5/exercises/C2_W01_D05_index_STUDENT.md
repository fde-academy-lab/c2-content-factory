# Which exercises does Friday run, and what do you hand in?

Kavya Nair set the day's question: can you rebuild the week alone on a raw export, and hold your
note when Marketing pushes? The lab is the day's exercise, and everything after it is practice with
someone listening.

| When | File | What it asks | You hand in |
|---|---|---|---|
| Morning, 120 minutes on the clock | `unguided/C2_W01_D05_lab_brief_STUDENT.md` with `../notebooks/C2_W01_D05_lab_STUDENT.ipynb` | Can you take a raw export to a note Anand would sign, alone, in two hours? | Three files written by the notebook's last cell |
| After the clock, the debrief | `../notebooks/C2_W01_D05_01_debrief_quarters_STUDENT.ipynb`, `02_debrief_values`, `03_debrief_segments` | Three chapters, one per place most rooms break, each on an invented export with a predict item at every level, and empty your-turn cells that run the same step on the lab file | Nothing; they open after the lab clock stops |
| Afternoon, round one in pairs | `guided/C2_W01_D05_rehearsal_brief_STUDENT.md` | Does your note hold when a partner playing Marketing pushes? | Nothing; you keep the feedback cards |
| Afternoon, after each defence | `guided/C2_W01_D05_feedback_card_STUDENT.md` | What did this defence hold, and what should change before Monday? | The card, to the defender |
| Afternoon, 40 minutes | `unguided/C2_W01_D05_timed_cases_STUDENT.md` | Which approach fits each of three design cases at Kalpa, sized how, and what would make you switch? | Nothing; the answers are spoken |
| The practice lab, after the day | `practice/C2_W01_D05_practice_STUDENT.md` | Can you rerun the step where you stalled, on the practice export? Nine of the sixteen items are design items | Sixteen letters in one line, and three numbers |

The practice solution opens at the end of the practice lab, in `solutions/`.
