## How is the sharpest push modelled before round one?

The brief carries Marketing's sharpest push on invented figures, the monsoon sale's Rs 3,040 against
Rs 2,900, with the segment table and the answer the evidence supports, worked in full. Model it once,
aloud, at S7, in four minutes, with a TA reading Marketing's lines. It is on neither pair list, so
every pair practises on pushes they have not seen answered.

A defender whose note carries Thursday's real figures gives the same answer with each invented figure
swapped for its real one: the blend Rs 3,395 against Rs 3,200, 6.1 percent more (the brief's Rs 3,040
against Rs 2,900, 4.8 percent); inside Retail-Plus Rs 4,850 for 30 against Rs 5,000 for 40, and
inside Retail-Core Rs 1,940 for 30 against Rs 2,000 for 60, 3.0 percent less each (the brief's
