## The writing task

Two hypotheses, one of which is not the reorder button, each with data somebody could pull this
week. Full credit needs the test to be specific enough to run.

**A strong pair**

> **Hypothesis 1.** The reorder button broke, so members who used to reorder stopped.
> **What settles it.** Reorder-button events per Retail-Plus member per week, for the six weeks
> before and the six after, against the same measure for Retail-Core.

> **Hypothesis 2.** A competitor's membership offer launched in the same window and took share of
> the repeat occasion.
> **What settles it.** Retail-Plus members who ordered in Q1 and not in Q2, cross-checked against
> whether they are still opening the app. Members who stopped buying and stopped visiting look like
> a competitor; members who still visit and do not buy look like the button.

**What loses credit:** a second hypothesis that is the first one reworded, or a test that needs data
nobody collects. "Ask the customers" is not a test unless you say who, how many and what you would
ask them.
