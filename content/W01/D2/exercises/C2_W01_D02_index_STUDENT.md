# Which exercises follow each chapter on Tuesday, and what question does each ask?

> "Q2 came in below Q1: are we losing customers, or are the ones we have buying less?"
>
> Meera Raghavan, CEO, Kalpa Retail

Every exercise today puts Meera Raghavan's question back to you. She is the CEO of Kalpa Retail, and
its Q2, July to September, came in below Q1, April to June, in booked revenue: every order placed, at
the price charged, before any cancellation or return. The day climbs that one case in six chapters,
and each piece of work below belongs to one chapter, to one of the afternoon's two cases, to the
practice lab or to tonight. An item marked Design asks for the best-fit approach, a sizing, the fact
that would switch it, or a second route, and 25 of the 58 items in the chapter sets, the two cases
and the lab are design items.
