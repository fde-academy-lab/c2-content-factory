The Typical-Number Bench

Four switches, one file, forty-four profiled Kalpa orders. Flip them and watch what happens to the number you were about to send. Nothing is typed and nothing is saved; close the tab and it resets. Keep this file, since it is the decision tool from today that you can reopen on any dataset argument later.

The switches

Which statistic do you send?
Three answers to "what is typical". They disagree once there is a tail.
Is the Rs 480,000 order in the file?
Order KR4232, real and retained. Taking it out is a decision somebody has to defend.
Which orders are you describing?
The whole file, or one segment at a time.
Below how many orders do you refuse to make a claim?
There is no correct answer here. Pick one you would defend out loud, then look at the table.
