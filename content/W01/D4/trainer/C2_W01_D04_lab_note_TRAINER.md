# How does the TA run Thursday's practice lab, what goes first when time is short, and where will learners stall?

**TRAINER ONLY.** For the TA who runs the practice lab after the afternoon block, from
`exercises/practice/C2_W01_D04_lab_STUDENT.md`, with the chapter sets in `exercises/unguided/` as its
stretch.

**Who needs the answer.** The TA, who has the room for about an hour after a full day and must leave
every learner with the four checks run once on cases they have not seen. A lab that spends its hour on
the chapter items the morning already tested sends the room home without the shuffle rerun and the
note, the two parts nothing else in the day repeats.

## How long does each part run, and who works alone?

The four problems are the core, about 60 minutes. The chapter sets' remaining 26 items are the
stretch: items 3 to 6 of chapters 1 to 4 and items 3 to 7 of chapters 5 and 6, about 25 minutes of
work for whoever finishes the core early, chapters 4 to 6's items first, and tonight's work for
everything left.

| Part | Minutes | Alone or pairs | What it tests |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Four p-value sentences | 10 | Alone | The sentence a share supports, in four new places |
| 2. Three unexplained gaps | 15 | Alone | Who got it, against whom, and what else changed |
| 3. The shuffle on Monday's sample | 20 | Pairs | The label shuffle on a file they know, and a definition that flips the verdict |
| 4. The whole day in one note | 15 | Pairs | Real, the count, the cause and "not yet" in one invented case |
| The core | 60 | | |
| Stretch: the chapter sets' items 3 onward, 26 in all | About 25, for early finishers; the rest goes home | Alone | The day's six chapters on new numbers, a third of the items asking for a design; answered in chat as one letter line per set |

## What goes first when the lab runs short?

Cut in this order, and stop as soon as the lab fits.

1. The stretch goes home whole as tonight's work: chapters 1 to 3's items go before chapters 4 to
   6's, since the morning's checkpoints already tested chapters 1 to 3.
2. Problem 1 shrinks to items 2 and 4, and problem 2 to item 6.
3. Problems 3 and 4 stay whole, because they are the only places the lab asks for the whole test and
   the whole note.

## Where do learners stall, and what is the one hint for each?

| Part | Where they stall | The one hint |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Item 1 reads "no effect" as the careful answer, and item 2 reads 99 percent sure as a fair summary | "In which world was the share counted? Say that world out loud first." |
| 2 | Item 7 picks the courier outright, because it sounds like the cause | "Two things changed in the same month. What would separate them in the data you already have?" |
| 3 | Step 1, marker 1: some keep Business because "all orders" sounds complete, and the two lakh-rupee orders then swamp every basket | "What is a basket for the head of Retail-Plus? Would a corporate order be one?" |
| 3 | Step 2: pairs report the booked result as the answer, and are surprised when the money kept flips it | "Which total did Monday say was the money kept? Count the orders behind it." |
| 4 | Notes that call the lift real because p = 0.02, and forget the 18 users | "Put the count beside the 9 percent, then ask who opted in." |
| Stretch | The design items: learners pick the most thorough-sounding option and skip the constraint in the stem | "What does the stem say you cannot do, or cannot spend? Cross out every option that breaks it." |

## What does a finished lab look like?

Seven letters for problems 1 and 2, five for problem 3, and a note for problem 4 that uses all three
facts: the opted-in users were already the most active, the comparison stands on 18 users, and
sending costs almost nothing, so the action is a random half for four weeks. Stretch letter lines are
a bonus, chapters 4 to 6 first. Read two notes aloud with their authors before the lab closes.

## What if the notebook will not run?

Problem 3 needs only the helper and the data file in `data/`. If a learner's Codespace fails, pair
them; the solution notebook opens read-only on GitHub with every output showing, and problems 1, 2
and 4 and the stretch need no notebook at all.
