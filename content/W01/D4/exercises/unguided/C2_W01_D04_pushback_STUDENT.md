# The second case: marketing pushes back

Forty minutes, in pairs, after the break. One partner builds the answer, the other plays the
marketing lead for the last eight minutes, and then you swap roles for the reply. The notebook is
`notebooks/C2_W01_D04_ex2_second_case_STUDENT.ipynb`: eight lettered choices in four moves.

> **The client asks.** "Your segment split is slicing the data until it says what you want. Exposed
> Retail-Plus members spent Rs 4,850 in August. That is far above the Rs 3,200 our unexposed customers
> averaged. The sale works. We repeat it for Diwali."
>
> The marketing lead, Kalpa Retail, in the growth review with Meera listening

The table is the campaign platform's August list: 160 Retail-Plus and Retail-Core customers under the
platform's own ids, with one average August spend for each group. It records who received the sale,
whatever the sale was aimed at.

## The four moves

| Move | The question you answer | Minutes |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Reproduce | Can you make marketing's Rs 4,850 and Rs 3,200 appear from the platform's list? | 7 |
| 2. Name the mismatch | Who sits in each of marketing's two groups, and what else differs between them? | 8 |
| 3. Rebuild | What do exposed and unexposed customers look like like for like, and in the same mix, with how many customers behind each? | 12 |
| 4. What it cost, and the offer | If the list's August spend is at list price, what did the exposed pay Kalpa, and what does a Diwali hold-back cost? | 13 |

## Rules for the pair

- Marketing's numbers are correct arithmetic. Your job is the comparison, and the arithmetic stands.
- Every average you quote carries the number of customers behind it.
- The list does not say whether its spend is counted before or after the 15 percent off, so the
  reply asks Marketing that question instead of assuming the answer.
- The reply offers a way to settle the question at Diwali; a reply that only says "you are wrong" has
  lost the room.
- The partner playing marketing may push once more after the reply, with any argument the table allows.
