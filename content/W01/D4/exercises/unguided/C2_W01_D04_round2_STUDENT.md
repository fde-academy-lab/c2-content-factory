# Round 2 set: real, and worth acting on?

Fifteen minutes, after the room has sized the Retail-Plus fall. Seven items. The numbers in items 1
to 4 are invented so that each tests the habit and none repeats the file's own result. Items 5 and 6
are two overclaiming sentences to rewrite honestly.

Post one line, seven letters in item order, no spaces:

```
Post exactly this shape: xxxxxxx
```

---

### Q1

An invented tier lost Rs 1,000 of delivered revenue per member across 30 members, in a quarter when
the company delivered Rs 1 crore. What share of the company's quarter is the fall?

a) 3 percent
b) 0.3 percent
c) 30 percent
d) 0.03 percent

### Q2

Two invented segments reach the growth review. Segment A moved Rs 5 lakh with a share of 0.40;
segment B moved Rs 30,000 with a share of 0.02. Which ranking does Kavya accept?

a) B first, since its share is the smaller of the two
b) Drop A from the review, since a share above 0.05 means its move did not happen
c) Rank by the percentage change, since percentages compare segments of different sizes fairly
d) Rank by rupees, and say beside each whether it beats chance

### Q3

A retention offer costs Rs 12,000 a quarter, against an invented fall of Rs 30,000 a quarter. What
share of the fall must it win back just to pay for itself in revenue?

a) 12 percent
b) 25 percent
c) 40 percent
d) 60 percent

### Q4

With two lakh orders a quarter, a Rs 5 difference in the average order comes back at a share of
0.02, and the head of pricing wants to reprice. What do you say?

a) Size it first: Rs 5 on two lakh orders is about Rs 10 lakh a quarter, then set that against what repricing costs
b) Reprice now, since a share of 0.02 settles the question
c) Ignore it, since five rupees can never matter to a company of Kalpa's size
d) Run a second test at one lakh orders, and act only if both runs come back under 0.05 on the same seed and the same week

### Q5

Which rewrite of "The Retail-Plus drop is statistically significant, so it is a crisis" survives
Kavya's review?

a) "The Retail-Plus drop is statistically significant and is therefore a crisis for the company on Monday."
b) "The Retail-Plus drop is 97 percent certain, and a crisis for Monday."
c) "The Retail-Plus fall beats the usual wobble; at Rs ___ a quarter, it is ___ percent of the company."
d) "The Retail-Plus drop is not significant, so it can safely be ignored until next year's growth review comes round."

### Q6

Which rewrite of "The shuffle proves the drop is real and large, so we must fund a fix" survives?

a) "The shuffle proves the drop is real, so the fix is justified whatever it costs the tier or the company."
b) "The drop is real and large, and every week without a fix costs Kalpa more, so the fix should start before Monday's review."
c) "The shuffle cannot say anything, so no fix should be considered at all."
d) "The drop beats chance; a fix pays only if it wins back more than its cost, so test it on part of the tier."
