### Q1

The app team reports that a new city's orders rose 60 percent, from 10 orders to 16, all from four
customers, and asks for a Rs 2 lakh launch budget. Coin flips on 26 orders make a rise that large in
about 16 of every 100 worlds. What goes back to the app team?

a) Fund it: 60 percent is the fastest rise on the page
b) Not yet: 16 in 100 is common, and four customers are too few
c) No: a new city never pays back in its first quarter
d) Fund half of it, because chance makes the rise only 16 times in 100

### Q2

An invented segment's orders rose from 10 in Q1 to 14 in Q2, a rise of 40 percent. If one Q2 order
had landed in Q1 instead, what would the rise have been?

a) About 18 percent
b) About 30 percent
c) About 36 percent
d) About 40 percent, unchanged

### Q3

A segment's rise stands on 9 customers, and new customers arrive at about 2 a quarter. A paid test
aimed at new customers would bring about 25 in two weeks for Rs 3,750. Which plan goes to Meera?

a) Wait about eleven quarters for thirty customers, since paid customers are not real
b) Neither, since a segment this small can never earn a budget line
c) Wait one quarter, since the next quarter's rise will settle it either way
d) The paid test: Rs 3,750 reaches thirty customers in two weeks; waiting takes years

### Q4

In an interview: 55 percent on 9 users against 38 percent on 2,000. Which do you trust, and how do
you say it?

a) 55 percent, since it is the higher of the two rates
b) Neither, since two rates can only be read after a shuffle
c) 38 percent as the estimate, and 55 percent as a lead to measure
d) 38 percent, since 2,000 users make it the true rate for everyone

### Q5

Kavya wants the exact chance share for two invented segments, one standing on 18 orders and one on
60. Which route fits each?

a) List every deal for both, since listing is exact
b) Flip coins for both, since flips are always enough
c) List every deal for the 18 orders, and flip coins for the 60
d) List every deal for the 60 orders, and flip coins for the 18

### Q6

Two invented segments reach Meera's page. X rose from 6 orders to 9, which is 50 percent; Y rose from
300 orders to 390, which is 30 percent. Which line goes in the note?

a) X is the growth story, because its rise is the larger
b) Y's rise as worth testing on its count, and X's as a lead
c) Neither, since both rises could be the usual wobble
d) Both, weighted equally, as each is computed on its own orders
