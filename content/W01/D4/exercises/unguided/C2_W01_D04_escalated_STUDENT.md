# The escalated case: one page to Meera by Monday

Sixty minutes, alone, at the start of the afternoon. You answer all three of Meera's questions and
ship the note. The notebook is `notebooks/C2_W01_D04_ex1_escalated_case_STUDENT.ipynb`: twelve
lettered choices in five parts, a check after each, and the note at the end.

> **The client asks.** "One: Retail-Plus is down, smaller than first reported. Real, or the wobble we
> see every quarter? Two: Student is up 40 percent; should I move budget there? Three: marketing ran a
> monsoon-sale discount for Retail-Plus in August, says it lifted revenue 6 percent, and wants to
> repeat it for Diwali. Did the discount work, or did those customers buy anyway? One page, two
> minutes. If the honest answer is 'we do not know yet', say so and tell me what would tell us."
>
> Meera Raghavan, CEO, Kalpa Retail

## The five parts

| Part | Meera's question, as you answer it | What you hand in | Minutes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Real? | Is the Retail-Plus fall larger than the usual wobble? | The share from 5,000 shuffles, seed 2026, and the sentence that survives Kavya | 10 |
| 2. Worth it? | How big is the fall, next to the company's quarter? | The fall in rupees a quarter and its share of the company | 8 |
| 3. Student | Can the 40 percent carry a budget decision? | The count behind the rate, never printed by the notebook, and how often chance makes the rise | 10 |
| 4. The discount | Did the monsoon sale lift revenue, and for whom? | The blended comparison, the comparison inside each segment, and the mix of the two groups | 17 |
| 5. The note | What does Meera read on Monday? | Claim, evidence, caveat and action for all three questions, under 200 words | 15 |

## Rules for the hour

- The data is the day's three files: the cleaned orders, the exposure table and the campaigns table.
  Nothing needs cleaning again.
- Delivered revenue is the money kept, per Monday's definition.
- Every number in the note carries its count or its denominator.
- "Not yet, and here is what would tell us" is a complete answer where the evidence says so.
- The support TA answers environment problems only.

## What you post

One line of twelve letters in the order of the notebook's markers, then your note pasted below it.

```
Post exactly this shape: xxxxxxxxxxxx
```

Read the note aloud to your partner before the debrief. If it runs past two minutes, cut the
evidence, never the caveat.
