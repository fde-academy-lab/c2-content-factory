# The escalated case: one page to Meera by Monday

Fifty minutes, alone, straight after chapter 6. You answer all three of Meera's questions, size the
Diwali hold-back on the list it acts on, and ship the note. The notebook is
`notebooks/C2_W01_D04_ex1_escalated_case_STUDENT.ipynb`: twelve lettered choices in five parts, a
check after each part, and the note at the end.

> **The client asks.** "One: Retail-Plus is down, smaller than first reported. Real, or the wobble we
> see every quarter? Two: Student is up 40 percent; should I move budget there? Three: marketing ran a
> monsoon-sale discount for Retail-Plus in August, says it lifted revenue 6 percent, and wants to
> repeat it for Diwali. Did the discount work, or did those customers buy anyway? One page, two
> minutes. If the honest answer is 'we do not know yet', say so and tell me what would tell us."
>
> Meera Raghavan, CEO, Kalpa Retail

## The five parts

| Part | Meera's question, as you answer it | What you hand in | Minutes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Real? | Is the Retail-Plus fall larger than the usual wobble? | The orders that count, the chance reference that fits the same members measured twice, and the share counted both ways | 10 |
| 2. Worth it? | How big is the fall next to the company's quarter, and who gets the offer first? | The fall in rupees a quarter, its share of the company, and the members a first test offers | 10 |
| 3. Student | Can the 40 percent carry a budget decision? | The count behind the rate, which the notebook never prints, and how often chance makes the rise | 8 |
| 4. The discount | Did the monsoon sale lift revenue, and for whom? | The customers who got it, the fair comparison, and the mix of the group who got it | 10 |
| 5. Diwali and the note | What does Meera read on Monday? | The hold-back sized on the platform's list, then claim, evidence, caveat and action for all three questions, under 200 words | 12 |

## Rules for the case

- The data is the day's three files: Finance's cleaned orders, the campaign platform's August list
  (the exposure table) and the campaigns table. Nothing needs cleaning again.
- The two lists hold different customers under different ids. Part 2's offer is sized on Finance's
  22 members, and part 5's hold-back on the platform's 70 Retail-Plus customers.
- Delivered revenue is the money kept, per Monday's definition.
- Every number in the note carries its count or its denominator, and the Retail-Plus share goes in
  with both directions beside it.
- "Not yet, and here is what would tell us" is a complete answer where the evidence says so.
- The support TA answers environment problems only.
