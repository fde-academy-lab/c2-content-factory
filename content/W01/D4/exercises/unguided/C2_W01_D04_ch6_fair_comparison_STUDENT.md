### Q1

An invented readout says a targeted segment delivered 120 percent more in its sale month than in the
month before, on a handful of orders each month. Which check comes first?

a) Rerun the before-and-after on revenue per order instead of revenue, since that removes the effect of the handful of orders
b) Put an untargeted segment beside it over the same two months, and count what each month stands on
c) Accept the rise, since 120 percent is too large for chance to make
d) Compare the sale month with the same month last year
