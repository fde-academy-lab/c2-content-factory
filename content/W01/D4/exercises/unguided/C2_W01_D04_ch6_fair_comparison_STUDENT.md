### Q1

An invented readout says a targeted segment delivered 120 percent more in its sale month than in the
month before, on a handful of orders each month. Which check comes first?

a) Rerun the before-and-after on revenue per order, so that the handful of orders stops mattering
b) Put an untargeted segment beside it over the same months, and count what each month stands on
c) Accept the rise, since a jump of 120 percent is far too large for chance to make
d) Set the sale month against the same month last year, which takes the season out

### Q3

### Q4

An invented chain runs a sale in every one of its northern stores, and the franchise owners refuse
any hold-back. The southern stores run no sale, and 24 months of revenue exist for both regions.
