# The fall edges past chance: is it big enough, in rupees against what a fix costs, to act on?

Chapter 2 set, six items, after chapter 2: items 1 and 2 run live in the chapter's last three
minutes; the rest are the practice lab's stretch or tonight's work.

> "So my tier really is slipping. What do I get to fix it?"
>
> The head of Retail-Plus, Kalpa Retail, before the growth review

Retail-Plus is Kalpa Retail's paid membership tier, and its 22 members delivered Rs 1,110 less each
in Q2 than in Q1, counting only the money Kalpa kept. Chapter 1 flipped each member's two quarters
with a coin, 5,000 times, to see how often chance alone makes a fall that large: 0.029 of those
chance-only worlds did, and 0.057 made a move that large in either direction, which the chapter read
as borderline. That share, the p-value, answers one question, and chapter 2 asked the next one: in
rupees, is the fall big enough to act on? A retention offer is a payment or perk meant to win back
members' spend, and its break-even is the share of the fall it must win back to pay for itself, its
cost divided by the fall. A range for the fall comes from resampling the members' own Q1 less Q2
differences many times, and it shows how small or how large the fall could really be. Kavya Nair, the
senior analyst on the team, reviews every line before it reaches Meera Raghavan, Kalpa Retail's CEO.

**Who needs the answer.** The head of Retail-Plus wants a retention budget, and Meera weighs it
against the quarter. A wrong call funds an offer that cannot pay back, or ignores a fall that keeps
draining the tier.

**The questions on the way.**

### Q1. Which half of a 40-member tier gets the first test of a retention offer?

An invented tier of 40 members fell Rs 800 each in a quarter, and a retention offer costs Rs 300 a
member, so it must win back 37.5 percent of the fall to pay for itself. Nobody has measured a
recovery, and the head of the tier agrees to test the offer on half the tier first. Which half gets
it?

a) A coin-chosen 20, so the two halves start alike
b) The 20 who fell most, since they have the most to win back
c) The 20 with the lowest customer ids, which is close to random
d) The 20 who spent most last quarter, since they matter most

### Q2. What does Kavya reply when the head of Retail-Plus calls the fall the company's biggest problem?

The head of Retail-Plus says, "The flips put our fall well outside the usual wobble, so it is the
company's biggest problem." Which reply does Kavya give?

a) Agree: a small share means the fall is the largest on the page
b) Agree, once a second seed gives the same share
c) Disagree: size its rupees against the company first
d) Disagree: the share is borderline, so the fall did not happen

### Q3. What does a range of Rs 150 to Rs 1,900 a member buy against a Rs 400 offer?

An invented range for a tier's fall runs from Rs 150 to Rs 1,900 a member around an estimate of
Rs 1,000, and a retention offer costs Rs 400 a member. What does the budget holder get?

a) The offer for every member, because most of the range sits above the cost
b) Nothing, because a range that wide supports no spending at all
c) A test on part of the tier first: the low end sits below the cost
d) The offer at the estimate's break-even of 40 percent, for every member

### Q4. In what order does the growth review take three findings with different rupees and shares?

Three invented findings reach the growth review. A moved Rs 5 lakh with a share of 0.40; B moved
Rs 30,000 with a share of 0.02; C moved Rs 2,000 with a share of 0.001. In what order does the
review take them?

a) C, B, A: the smallest share first, since it is the surest
b) A, B, C: by rupees, each with its share said beside it
c) B, C, A: the two that beat chance first, then the rest
d) A alone, since the other two are too small to discuss

### Q5. What does the budget meeting decide on a Rs 50,000 fix for a Rs 60,000 fall?

An invented tier's fall is Rs 60,000 a quarter, and a fix costs Rs 50,000. Last year the same fix,
on members like these, won back 70 percent of a similar fall. What does the budget meeting decide?

a) Fund it: 70 percent is a high recovery for any fix
b) Test it on half the tier first, since the rate is last year's
c) Fund it: the fall is large and the fix is proven
d) Not as priced: it needs 83 percent back and wins back 70

### Q6. What happens to a Rs 20 gap's share, and to the gap, when 100 orders a quarter become 20,000?

An invented Rs 20 gap between two quarters of different orders gives a share of 0.43 on 100 orders
a quarter. What happens to the share, and to the gap, on 20,000 orders a quarter?

a) The share falls toward zero, and the gap stays Rs 20
b) The share stays near 0.43, while the gap grows with the orders
c) Both fall, since the orders dilute the gap
d) The share rises, since more orders add more noise
