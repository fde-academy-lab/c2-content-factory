# Unguided: cause, or coincidence?

Alone, no hints. Six short vignettes, each from a business somebody could work in tomorrow.

Post one line with your six letters, then rewrite the claim at the end.

```
Post exactly this shape: xxxxxx
```

---

## Q1. Kalpa Health emails a reminder and no-shows fall from 14 to 11 percent

Which one?

a) The reminder works and should be rolled out to every clinic immediately
b) The reminder cannot have worked, because no-shows are driven by weather
c) Ask who received the reminder, and how they differ from who did not
d) Ask for a longer time series, since two points cannot show a trend

---

## Q2. Retail-Plus members who use the app spend 40 percent more than those who do not

Which one?

a) The app drives spending, so install prompts should go to every member
b) Heavier spenders were more likely to install the app already
c) The comparison is fine because both groups are Retail-Plus members
d) Spending drives app installs, so the arrow points the other way entirely

---

## Q3. A store's revenue rose the month after it was refitted

Which one?

a) The refit worked, since revenue is the measure the refit was aimed at
b) The refit failed, because a rise of one month is within normal variation
c) Ask what else happened in that month, and what comparable stores did
d) Ask for the refit's cost, so the return on it can be worked out first

---

## Q4. Exposed customers spent 6 percent more, and every segment spent 3 percent less

Which one?

a) One of the two figures has been computed incorrectly somewhere
b) The exposed group has a different mix of segments from the control
c) The segments are too small for their individual numbers to be trusted
d) The campaign worked for some customers and not for others in the mix

---

## Q5. Kalpa Connect's churn fell after a price cut, and a competitor left the market that month

Which one?

a) The price cut worked, and the competitor's exit is a coincidence
b) The competitor's exit explains it, so the price cut can be reversed
c) Compare against the previous year's same month to remove the effect
d) Both changed at once, so this data cannot separate them

---

## Q6. A team randomises a discount within one segment and finds no difference

Which one?

a) The discount does not work for that segment, on this evidence
b) The experiment failed and should be re-run with a larger sample
c) Randomisation was the wrong design for a question about discounts
d) The discount works and the effect was too small for this test to see

---

## Writing task

Marketing's claim, as they wrote it:

> "The monsoon sale lifted revenue six percent. We should repeat it for Diwali."

Rewrite it so that every word is defensible on the evidence you have. Then write one sentence saying
what would have to be done differently next time for the claim they wanted to make to be
answerable.

Under sixty words for both.
