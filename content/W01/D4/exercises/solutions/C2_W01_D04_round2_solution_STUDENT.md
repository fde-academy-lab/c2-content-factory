## The idea being tested

Two calls, two measurements. The shuffle says whether a gap beats chance. Rupees against the whole
company and against the cost of acting say whether it deserves money. Every item puts one of those
calls where the other belongs and asks you to separate them.

## The part worth arguing about

Item 4. The slide's invented example called a five-rupee gap trivial, and some pairs will choose c.
At two lakh orders it is about Rs 10 lakh a quarter, which could matter; the point is that the share
cannot tell you, and only the size against the cost can.

## Where the pattern lives in production

A product team with millions of users finds that almost every change is "significant". The teams that
ship well write the size in money and the cost of the change beside every p-value, and rank their
backlog by the first two columns rather than the third.
