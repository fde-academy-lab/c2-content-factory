# Solution: chapter 5 set, the note that may say not yet

Answers: 1b 2d 3a 4c 5d 6c

## The idea being tested

A CEO's note carries each number with its partner (a share with its world, a rupee figure with its
whole, a rate with its count, a lift with its groups) and a decision in the same place. The design
items ask which form fits Meera's request, which fact would switch it to a dashboard, and what the
tracing route is for. The other items ask whether a line passes the audit and how "not yet" is said
so that it still answers.

## The part worth arguing about

Item 2, option c. A long note is a real problem, and some pairs reach for the dashboard to solve it.
The fix for a long note is cutting it back to four parts per question; the dashboard earns its place
only when the same metrics come back every week and the bases stop changing.

## Where the pattern lives in production

Amazon replaced slide presentations in its meetings with narratively structured six-page memos, read
silently at the start of each meeting. The discipline is the same at any length: every claim carries
its evidence and its caveat, and the reader leaves with a decision instead of a deck.
