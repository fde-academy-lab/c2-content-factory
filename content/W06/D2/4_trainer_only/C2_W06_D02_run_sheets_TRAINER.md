pointer, a worked parallel). A group opens one only with a member of staff present, at the
checkpoint or when it calls one of you, and writes the hint and why it was needed in the challenges
log. Before any hint, ask the stage's question back. `4_trainer_only/C2_W06_D02_starter_repo_notes_TRAINER.md`
says which hint unseals when.

**The AI lanes, enforced the same way by all three of you.**

If the expert is not confirmed for Friday, TA1 chairs room 1, and the retakes move to TA2 on the
build floor. Every group's cold run takes 15 minutes inside block two minutes 90 to 150, in any order
the pairs agree, as long as the partner group can watch it.

**The freeze.** At block two minute 90 every group pushes and tags its commit `build2-freeze`. After
the tag only the slides and the README may change. TA2 asks the evaluator of each group three
questions as the tag lands:

| Question | A pass |
|---|---|
| Does the breadcrumb notebook run top to bottom from a fresh Codespace on the tagged commit? | Yes, with the run's start and end written in the challenges log |
| Does the README's first line carry your number and its caveat? | The list size, the error bill and the honesty line |
| Has each of you said slide 1 aloud against a timer since Thursday? | Three names |

**The cold run.** The evaluator opens a fresh Codespace on the tagged commit, installs, runs the
tests, runs the breadcrumb notebook top to bottom, opens the app and scores one customer. The
partner group records it on a phone and writes "ran cold" or "stopped at __" in the challenges log;
TA1 copies the outcome into the workbook's Groups sheet. A run that stops on something outside the
code (a missing install step, a wrong path in the README) gets one rerun after that fix; a run that
stops in the code is recorded as "stopped", and the group presents Saturday's demo from its executed
notebook if the live app stops again.

**Dry run 2.** Twelve minutes on cards, from a fresh start of the demo, recorded by the partner
group on a phone. The presenting group watches the first ninety seconds back before it leaves the
room. Pairs run 15 and 15 minutes; the trio starts at minute 135 and runs 15 each.

**The passport retake.** Five minutes per learner with TA1: two numbers drawn from the passport,
each recomputed live from the repository. "Held" or "still not yet" goes in the workbook's Mock R2
sheet; Saturday's questions are then a second exposure, never a first.

## What moves when a day goes wrong?

| What happens | What you do |
|---|---|
| A role-player cannot join Tuesday's calls | The two others take the third room's groups at the end of their own, into their reserve minutes; room A's G5 moves to room B's reserve |
| A group's Codespace will not start on Tuesday | It works on paper through block two; TA2 fixes it in the lab; the data's shape must print before Wednesday's checkpoint |
| A gate overruns | The assessor cuts the gate's questions, never the viva; the slot after starts on time from the reserve |
| A learner misses the gate | TA1 runs that learner's viva alone in Thursday's block one between minutes 110 and 180, five minutes, from the same gate file |
| A cold run stops on Friday | One rerun if the cause sits outside the code; otherwise "stopped" goes in the Groups sheet, and the group keeps its executed notebook ready as Saturday's fallback |
| The expert is late on Friday | Up to 20 minutes: PH fills the gap with Saturday's briefing (the question types, the time cards, the drawn presenter), and GD rounds 1 to 3 start that much later, into block one's reserve. Later than that: the GD rounds start on time with TA1 chairing room 1, and the showcase runs when the expert arrives for every group not in a round |
| Saturday runs late | Cut in the order in Saturday's box; never move a group out of its card's pair |
