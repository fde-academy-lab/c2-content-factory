# How do we turn this template into our twelve-minute talk?

Open `C2_W06_D02_presentation_template_STUDENT.pptx` in PowerPoint, Google Slides or LibreOffice
Impress and fill every blank (`__`) with a number from your own repository. The `.md` file beside it
is the same deck as text, kept so you can read it on GitHub. Each slide's notes say who speaks, for
how long and what to say first.

## Who says what, minute by minute?

| Minutes | Slide | Who | What |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 1:30 | 1 | Drawn by the panel | The recommendation and its error bill in one sentence, then situation, complication, question |
| 1:30 to 3:00 | 2 | Decision owner | What each error costs, and the metric that follows |
| 3:00 to 6:00 | 3 | Builder | The live demo: one customer, the reason, the cost, then the card's number changed |
| 6:00 to 8:00 | 4 | Evaluator | A against B on the error bill, and why you chose |
| 8:00 to 9:30 | 5 | Evaluator | How honest the number is: doing nothing, the spread, the trap you caught |
| 9:30 to 11:00 | 6 | Decision owner | What the POC proved and what an MVP still needs |
| 11:00 to 12:00 | 7 | Builder | What would change your call, your top risk, your ask |

Slide 8 is the backup: the five numbers any of you may be asked to recompute live.

## What makes the first ninety seconds work?

- The first word anyone says is "Rohan", and the first sentence is the "Say this first" line on
  slide 1 with your numbers in it.
- The problem gets one minute, inside slide 1, and never a slide of its own.
- The panel draws who presents slide 1, so all three of you rehearse it with a phone timer until it
  fits in ninety seconds.

## How do we rehearse?

| When | Audience | What changes after it |
|---|---|---|
| Thursday, dry run 1 | Your red-team partner group, holding the time cards | A part that ran over loses words; nobody speeds up |
| Friday, dry run 2, from a fresh Codespace | Your partner group, recording on a phone | You watch the first ninety seconds back together |

The time cards: green at two minutes left, amber at one minute left, red at twelve minutes.
