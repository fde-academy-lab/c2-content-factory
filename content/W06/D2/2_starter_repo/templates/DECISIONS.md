# Decisions: what did our group choose, and why?

One row per decision, written the day it is made. A decision is any choice another group could have
made differently: a row kept or set aside, a column kept or dropped, how a column is written, the
model, the threshold or the list size. Saturday's panel may ask any member about any row, so write
the reason in words every member can say aloud.
